# Master Service Agreement — Prairie Wind Manufacturing

## 1. Parties
The Master Service Agreement — Prairie Wind Manufacturing is between Ridgeline Freight, LLC and Prairie Wind Manufacturing.

Ridgeline Freight, LLC (hereinafter "Carrier" or "Provider")
1432 Quarrystone Avenue
Chicago, IL 60632

Prairie Wind Manufacturing (hereinafter "Customer")
Des Moines, IA

## 2. Agreement Date and Term
### 2.1 Effective Date
This Agreement is effective as of 05/01/2026.

### 2.2 Term
The Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until terminated in accordance with Section 10 (Term and Termination). The parties may amend or extend the term only by written agreement signed by authorized representatives of each party.

## 3. Definitions
For purposes of this Agreement the following capitalized terms have the meanings set forth below:
- "Agreement" means this Master Service Agreement and any attachments, exhibits, schedules, and rate sheets incorporated by reference.
- "Services" means transportation, logistics, handling, and related services to be provided by Carrier for Customer's shipments of metal components and assemblies.
- "Shipment" means any load tendered by Customer to Carrier for transport under this Agreement.
- "Rate Sheet" means the written schedule of rates and charges referenced in Section 5 (Rates and Payment Terms).
- "Customer Data" means information provided by Customer in connection with performance of the Services.

Other terms used in this Agreement have the meanings commonly ascribed in the transportation industry unless otherwise defined herein.

## 4. Scope of Services and Service Levels
### 4.1 Services
Carrier shall provide motor carrier, freight handling, and related logistics services for Customer's shipments of metal components and assemblies. Carrier agrees to perform Services in a professional manner consistent with industry standards and applicable law.

### 4.2 Service Levels
Carrier will use commercially reasonable efforts to meet service levels agreed in writing on the applicable Rate Sheet or in shipment instructions. Specific transit commitments for individual lanes, if any, will be set out in the Rate Sheet or shipment tender documents.

### 4.3 Warehousing
Customer may tender shipments to Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Twin Cities located at:
Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Twin Cities
402 Meridian Park Drive
Minneapolis, MN 55411
Carrier operates the warehouse with loading docks and will comply with the warehouse's posted policies for inbound and outbound appointments.

## 5. Rates and Payment Terms
### 5.1 Rates
Rates and charges for Services will be as set forth in the Rate Sheet, which is incorporated by reference. The Rate Sheet controls in the event of any inconsistency with other non-executed documents.

### 5.2 Invoicing
Carrier will invoice Customer for Services in accordance with the Rate Sheet and applicable accessorial provisions. Invoices will reference shipment identifiers and supporting documents reasonably required by Customer.

### 5.3 Payment Terms
Payment terms are Net 60. All payments are due in United States currency and shall be made without setoff or deduction except as expressly provided in this Agreement. Late payment may result in suspension of Services under reasonable procedures and subject to dispute provisions in Section 8 (Claims).

## 6. Accessorials and Exceptions
Carrier's accessorial charges are set forth on the Rate Sheet. Accessorial fees apply in accordance with the Rate Sheet and applicable procedures.

Detention charges apply after free time, except for shipments to Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Twin Cities (warehouse policy applies).

Carrier will provide Customer with reasonably detailed documentation of any accessorials charged. Any exception to standard accessorial practice will be documented in writing.

## 7. Delivery, Late Delivery Remedies and Penalties
### 7.1 Delivery
Carrier will use commercially reasonable efforts to deliver shipments in accordance with the agreed instructions. Carrier will notify Customer of material delays reasonably promptly.

### 7.2 Late Delivery Remedies
If Carrier fails to deliver an affected Shipment within the agreed service window and such failure is not excused under Force Majeure or other express provisions of this Agreement, Customer may pursue remedies as set forth herein and in the Rate Sheet.

### 7.3 Penalty
For late deliveries, Carrier shall be subject to a late delivery penalty of 2.0% per week of the affected invoice amount, applied to the invoice for the Shipment to which the delay relates and subject to documentation requirements and the dispute procedures in Section 8.

## 8. Claims, Cargo Liability and Insurance
### 8.1 Claims Procedure
Claims for loss, damage, or delay must be submitted in writing to Carrier with reasonable supporting documentation. Carrier will acknowledge receipt and commence an investigation. The parties will cooperate in loss mitigation and investigation.

### 8.2 Cargo Liability Cap
Carrier's liability for loss of, or damage to, Customer's cargo shall be limited to $25,000.00 per shipment, except to the extent otherwise required by applicable statute. This limitation is the exclusive remedy against Carrier for cargo loss or damage unless the parties expressly agree in writing to an alternate value declaration and supplemental charge on the Rate Sheet.

### 8.3 Insurance
Carrier will maintain, at its expense, insurance customary for the performance of the Services and as required by law. Upon Customer request, Carrier will furnish certificates of insurance and evidence of coverage reasonably acceptable to Customer.

## 9. Indemnification and Limitation of Liability
### 9.1 Indemnification by Carrier
Carrier will defend, indemnify and hold Customer harmless from third-party claims arising out of Carrier's negligence or willful misconduct in performing the Services, subject to the limitations of liability set forth in this Agreement.

### 9.2 Indemnification by Customer
Customer will defend, indemnify and hold Carrier harmless from claims arising from Customer's noncompliance with law, improper packaging, hazardous materials misdeclaration, or breach of this Agreement.

### 9.3 Limitation of Liability
Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement or required by applicable law, neither party will be liable to the other for consequential, incidental, special, or punitive damages. Monetary liability for cargo loss or damage is subject to the Cargo Liability Cap in Section 8.2.

## 10. Term and Termination
### 10.1 Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement with 45 days prior written notice to the other party. Termination shall not relieve either party of obligations incurred prior to termination.

### 10.2 Immediate Termination
Either party may terminate immediately for material breach that is not cured within a reasonable period after written notice, or for insolvency of the other party.

### 10.3 Effect of Termination
Upon termination, Carrier will complete all accepted Shipment tenders and Customer will pay for Services performed and prepaid but unused services as set out in the Rate Sheet. Sections that by their nature should survive termination will survive.

## 11. Confidentiality and Data Protection
Each party will maintain in confidence and not disclose to third parties confidential business information received from the other party, except as necessary to perform the Services or by legal compulsion. Confidential information does not include information that is publicly known without breach, independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party.

Customer Data will be used by Carrier only to perform Services and for aggregation of operational metrics in anonymized form. Each party will implement reasonable administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect confidential information.

## 12. Force Majeure; Excused Performance
Neither party will be liable for failure or delay in performing obligations under this Agreement to the extent caused by events beyond the party's reasonable control, including acts of God, labor disputes, government action, and severe weather, provided the affected party notifies the other and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

## 13. Governing Law and Notices
### 13.1 Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois; venue in Cook County, Illinois.

### 13.2 Notices
All notices required or permitted under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered to the addresses in Section 1 or to such other address as a party designates in writing. Notices are effective upon receipt.

## 14. General Provisions
### 14.1 Independent Contractors
The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, agency, or joint venture relationship.

### 14.2 Assignment
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except that Carrier may subcontract carriage to third-party carriers consistent with industry practice.

### 14.3 Entire Agreement
This Agreement, together with the Rate Sheet and any incorporated documents, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by authorized representatives.

### 14.4 Severability
If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions will remain effective.

### 14.5 Waiver
A party's failure to enforce any provision of this Agreement does not constitute a waiver of future enforcement of that or any other provision.
