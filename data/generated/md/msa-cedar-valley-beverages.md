# Master Service Agreement — Cedar Valley Beverages

## 1. Parties
### 1.1 Agreement Parties
This Master Service Agreement — Cedar Valley Beverages is between Ridgeline Freight, LLC ("Carrier" or "Provider") and Cedar Valley Beverages ("Customer").

### 1.2 Carrier Information
Ridgeline Freight, LLC  
1432 Quarrystone Avenue  
Chicago, IL 60632

### 1.3 Customer Information
Cedar Valley Beverages  
Customer is engaged in the Beverage industry and will tender shipments of bottled non-alcoholic beverages.

## 2. Agreement Date and Term
### 2.1 Effective Date
This Agreement is effective as of 07/01/2026.

### 2.2 Term
This Agreement will remain in effect until terminated pursuant to its termination provisions. Either party may terminate the Master Service Agreement — Cedar Valley Beverages with 120 days' prior written notice.

## 3. Scope of Services
### 3.1 Services Provided
Carrier will provide freight transportation, related logistics coordination, and ancillary services as agreed in individual shipment confirmations or statements of work. Services will be performed consistent with industry practices and this Agreement.

### 3.2 Authority and Independent Contractor
Carrier operates as an independent contractor. Carrier has authority to select means, methods and personnel necessary to perform the Services, subject to Customer instructions that are consistent with this Agreement and applicable law.

### 3.3 Compliance with Law
Each party shall comply with applicable laws, rules and regulations applicable to its performance and obligations under this Agreement.

## 4. Rates, Billing and Payment
### 4.1 Rates
Rates for specific shipments, lanes and services shall be as set forth on the Rate Sheet executed by the parties and incorporated by reference into this Agreement.

### 4.2 Invoicing
Carrier will submit invoices in accordance with the billing procedures set out in the Rate Sheet or as otherwise agreed in writing. Invoices shall reference the applicable shipment identification and any accessorial charges.

### 4.3 Payment Terms
Payment terms are Net 90. Customer shall pay undisputed invoices in accordance with the Net 90 payment term. If an invoice or portion thereof is disputed, the parties will cooperate promptly to resolve the dispute and payment of undisputed amounts will continue under the Net 90 payment term.

### 4.4 Set-Off
Neither party may unilaterally set off amounts not expressly agreed to in writing. Any claim giving rise to a right of set-off must be documented and resolved in accordance with the claims procedure in this Agreement.

## 5. Accessorial Charges and Exceptions
### 5.1 Accessorial Charges
Standard accessorial charges will be set forth on the Rate Sheet. Carrier will notify Customer of any applicable accessorials at or before tender unless otherwise agreed.

### 5.2 TONU Exception
TONU waived only if Customer cancels 48 hours or more before pickup; otherwise TONU applies.

### 5.3 Additional Services
Requests for services outside the Scope of Services will be priced and authorized in writing prior to performance.

## 6. Service Levels, Remedies and Penalties
### 6.1 Service Levels
Carrier will use commercially reasonable efforts to meet agreed pickup and delivery windows and performance metrics set out in the Rate Sheet or shipment confirmations.

### 6.2 Late Delivery Remedies
If a shipment fails to meet agreed delivery requirements, remedies will be limited to those set forth in this Agreement and the Rate Sheet. The Agreement imposes a late delivery penalty of 1.0% per week of the affected invoice amount. Such remedy is in lieu of other consequential damages for late delivery, except as otherwise required by law.

### 6.3 Measurement and Calculation
Application of remedies for failure to meet service levels will be calculated against the affected invoice in accordance with procedures agreed in the Rate Sheet or otherwise agreed in writing.

## 7. Claims and Cargo Liability
### 7.1 Cargo Liability Cap
Carrier's liability for cargo loss or damage shall not exceed $75,000.00 per shipment unless otherwise agreed in writing.

### 7.2 Filing Claims
Claims for loss, damage or shortage shall be made in writing and supported by documentation customary in the industry. The parties will cooperate in investigation and adjustment of claims.

### 7.3 Limitation of Liability
Except as expressly provided in this Agreement, neither party shall be liable for consequential, special or punitive damages arising out of or related to this Agreement, to the fullest extent permitted by law.

## 8. Insurance and Indemnification
### 8.1 Insurance
Each party shall maintain insurance policies customary and appropriate for its business operations and for the performance of its obligations under this Agreement. Carrier shall maintain cargo insurance and liability insurance sufficient to cover its obligations under this Agreement.

### 8.2 Indemnification
Each party shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the other party from third-party claims arising from its negligent acts or willful misconduct in connection with this Agreement, subject to the limitations and caps set forth in this Agreement.

## 9. Confidentiality
### 9.1 Confidential Information
Each party will treat the other party's confidential information as confidential and will not disclose it except as necessary to perform obligations under this Agreement or as required by law. Confidential information does not include information that is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement.

### 9.2 Use and Return
Confidential information disclosed under this Agreement will be used only for the purposes of performing hereunder. Upon termination of this Agreement, each party will return or destroy the other party's confidential information as requested.

## 10. Termination
### 10.1 Termination for Convenience
Subject to the Term and Termination provisions in this Agreement, either party may terminate this Agreement in accordance with the notice provisions set forth herein. Either party may terminate the Master Service Agreement — Cedar Valley Beverages with 120 days' prior written notice.

### 10.2 Termination for Cause
A party may terminate for material breach that remains uncured following reasonable prior written notice and an opportunity to cure. Termination for cause will not relieve a breaching party of obligations accrued prior to termination.

### 10.3 Effect of Termination
Upon termination, the parties will cooperate to wind down operations in an orderly manner and to complete or transfer outstanding shipments and obligations in progress, subject to applicable payment and liability provisions.

## 11. Force Majeure
Neither party will be liable for delays or failures to perform caused by events beyond the party's reasonable control, including acts of God, labor disputes, governmental actions, civil disturbance, or other events that materially impede performance. The affected party will notify the other promptly and take reasonable steps to mitigate the effects.

## 12. Governing Law and Venue
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois; venue in Cook County, Illinois.

## 13. Notices
### 13.1 Method
All notices required under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, courier, or other mutually agreed method to the addresses for each party, or to such other address as a party designates by written notice.

### 13.2 Contact Information
Carrier: Ridgeline Freight, LLC, 1432 Quarrystone Avenue, Chicago, IL 60632.  
Customer: Cedar Valley Beverages, Cedar Rapids, IA.

## 14. Miscellaneous
### 14.1 Entire Agreement
This Agreement, the Rate Sheet and any executed attachments constitute the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersede all prior agreements and understandings relating to such subject matter.

### 14.2 Amendments
This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument executed by authorized representatives of both parties.

### 14.3 Severability
If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions will remain in full force and effect.

### 14.4 Assignment
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party’s prior written consent, except that Carrier may delegate performance to subcontractors or carriers consistent with Carrier’s obligations and this Agreement.

### 14.5 Relationship of the Parties
The parties are independent and nothing in this Agreement will be construed to create a partnership, joint venture, employment or agency relationship.
