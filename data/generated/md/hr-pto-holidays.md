# Employee Handbook — Paid Time Off and Holidays

## 1. Purpose
This Employee Handbook — Paid Time Off and Holidays describes Ridgeline Freight, LLC’s approach to paid time off, observed holidays, eligibility, accrual, carryover, requests, approvals and exceptions. The policy exists to support employee health, work-life balance and operational continuity while establishing clear expectations for all employees of Ridgeline Freight, LLC, headquartered at 1432 Quarrystone Avenue, Chicago, IL 60632. Ridgeline Freight, LLC was founded in 2009 and operates warehouses including Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Chicago West and Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Joliet.

## 2. Scope and Eligibility
This policy applies to all employees of Ridgeline Freight, LLC, subject to applicable law and to any separate agreements that may supersede parts of this handbook. Employees become eligible for PTO after 90 days of continuous employment. Temporary arrangements and contractor engagements are subject to separate agreements and are not covered by this policy unless explicitly stated in writing.

## 3. Policy Statement
Ridgeline Freight, LLC provides paid time off and paid holidays to eligible employees as part of the total compensation package. The Company seeks to balance employee needs with operational requirements at our facilities, including Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Chicago West located at 2107 Northwind Terrace, Chicago, IL 60639, and Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Joliet located at 88 Kestrel Loop, Joliet, IL 60433. This policy is administered in a uniform manner and in accordance with applicable law.

## 4. PTO Accrual
Employees accrue PTO at a rate of 0.58 days per biweekly pay period, and PTO accrual is prorated during the first 12 months of employment, except as otherwise required by law. Accrual begins once an employee meets eligibility requirements and is reflected on payroll records. Accruals are subject to adjustment for unpaid leaves and for separations in accordance with applicable law.

### 4.1 Accrual Recording and Availability
PTO balances are tracked in the Company’s timekeeping system and are available for review by employees through the usual internal channels. Accrued but unused PTO remains the property of the employee, subject to the carryover limits and payout rules contained in this document and to any state or local legal requirements.

## 5. Annual Accrual
Employees are eligible for 15 days per year under the Paid Time Off and Holidays policy. The annual accrual amount governs the maximum entitlement for a full year of employment before adjustments for prorations, unpaid leave, or other legal requirements. For part-year service or other prorated circumstances, the Company will apply the accrual and proration rules described in this handbook.

## 6. Carryover Policy
The maximum PTO carryover from one year to the next is 5 days. Employees should plan time off during the year; however, limited carryover of unused PTO is permitted to provide flexibility. Carryover beyond the limit stated in this policy will not be permitted unless required by law or approved in writing by Human Resources under exceptional operational circumstances.

## 7. Holidays
The Company observes 10 paid holidays per year. Observed holidays apply to operations at Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Chicago West and Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Joliet, unless alternative arrangements are communicated in advance. Holiday schedules and any adjustments due to operational needs will be provided by the Company in advance when practicable.

### 7.1 Holiday Pay and Scheduling
Holiday pay eligibility and scheduling will follow the Company’s payroll and scheduling practices. Employees required to work on an observed holiday may receive alternative time off or pay in accordance with applicable Company policy and law. Requests for holiday time off should be made in accordance with the procedures in Section 8.

## 8. Procedures: Requests, Approvals, and Use
To ensure consistent application of PTO and holiday benefits, employees and managers must follow these procedures.

1. Requesting PTO
   - Employees should submit PTO requests through the Company’s approved request system or by following manager guidance, with reasonable prior notice when possible.
   - Requests for foreseeable PTO should be submitted in accordance with department practices to allow for coverage planning.

2. Approvals and Conflicts
   - Supervisors will consider operational needs, staffing levels, and fairness when approving PTO.
   - When multiple requests conflict, supervisors will resolve conflicts using objective criteria consistent with business needs.

3. Unplanned Absences
   - For unexpected illness or emergency, employees should notify their supervisor as soon as reasonably possible and follow any departmental call-in procedures.

4. Documentation
   - The Company may request documentation for certain types of leave when permitted by law or when necessary to verify eligibility for PTO.

5. Payouts and Separation
   - Payout of accrued PTO at separation will be governed by applicable state law and Company policy. Employees should consult Human Resources for details relevant to their situation.

## 9. Exceptions and Proration
PTO accrual is prorated during the first 12 months of employment. This exception applies to accrual for new hires and to any other circumstances where prorated accrual is appropriate, except as otherwise required by law. Additional exceptions may be granted only by Human Resources in writing and must be consistent with legal obligations and Company objectives.

## 10. Compliance, At-Will Employment, Governing Law, and Venue
Employment at Ridgeline Freight, LLC is on an at-will basis unless expressly stated otherwise in a separate written agreement signed by an authorized Company officer. Nothing in this handbook creates a contract of employment for any specific duration.

This policy will be administered in accordance with applicable law. To the extent governing law is specified, the State of Illinois law governs interpretation and enforcement of this policy, and venue for any disputes will be Cook County, Illinois. The Company will comply with all federal, state and local laws that may modify or supersede portions of this policy.

## 11. Manager and Employee Responsibilities
Managers are responsible for:
- Communicating this policy to employees.
- Approving PTO requests consistent with operational needs.
- Maintaining accurate records of time off and coordinating coverage.

Employees are responsible for:
- Requesting time off in a timely manner.
- Managing PTO balances and using PTO in accordance with Company rules.
- Complying with call-in and documentation procedures for unplanned absences.

## 12. Notices, Force Majeure, and Entire Agreement
Notices under this policy will be provided electronically or in writing by the Company to employees at the usual Company address on file. The Company will not be liable for failure to meet obligations caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including force majeure events. This document, together with any written agreements expressly referenced herein, constitutes the entire Company policy on paid time off and holidays and supersedes prior written or unwritten PTO and holiday policies, subject to any separate agreements required by law.
