# SOP — Outbound Pick, Pack and Ship

## 1. Purpose
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) establishes requirements and procedures for outbound pick, pack, staging, and ship activities performed by and on behalf of Ridgeline Freight, LLC at its Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Joliet facility. The SOP is intended to ensure timely, accurate, and auditable outbound shipments, reduce accessorial exposure, and define responsibilities for internal staff and contracted carriers.

## 2. Scope
This SOP applies to all outbound pick, pack and ship operations executed at Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Joliet, 88 Kestrel Loop, Joliet, IL 60433 and to all carriers, customers, and internal personnel involved in those operations. It governs scheduling, cutoffs, loading, accessorials, escalation, recordkeeping, and performance measurement related to outbound shipments from that location. Ridgeline Freight, LLC headquarters: 1432 Quarrystone Avenue, Chicago, IL 60632.

## 3. Definitions
- Customer: The party that arranges shipment of commodity. Example customer referenced in this SOP: Lakeshore Retail Group.
- Carrier: The motor carrier or broker responsible for pickup and transit of outbound freight.
- Pick: Selection of inventory items to fulfill a customer order.
- Pack: Secure packaging and labeling required to prepare goods for shipment.
- Staging: Placement of packed units in a designated outbound area pending carrier loading.
- TONU: Truck Ordered Not Used; the condition where a carrier arrives for a scheduled pickup and the load is not tendered.
- Detention: Charges assessed when a carrier is delayed beyond allowed free time at a loading location.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities
### 4.1 Ridgeline Freight, LLC — Operations
- Receive and validate outbound orders.
- Schedule picks and manage staging areas at Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Joliet.
- Provide carriers with access to loading docks and required documentation.
- Monitor pick status and escalate exceptions per Section 8.

### 4.2 Warehouse Staff (Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Joliet)
- Execute picks, pack goods to specification, and move units to staging.
- Maintain outbound staging accuracy and load sequencing.
- Complete outbound documentation and record pick/pack times.

### 4.3 Carrier
- Arrive to scheduled dock appointment ready to load.
- Adhere to loading procedures and provide proof of pickup.
- Be responsible for cargo while the carrier has possession.

### 4.4 Sales & Commercial
- Coordinate customer requirements and special instructions with Operations.
- Communicate billing and accessorial policies to customers such as Lakeshore Retail Group.

### 4.5 Finance
- Apply rates and accessorial charges to customer billing.
- Reconcile disputed accessorials with Operations and Sales.

## 5. Step-by-step Procedure
1. Order Receipt and Validation
   - Sales or customer portal submits pick, pack, and staging requests to Operations.
   - Validate order details, SKUs, quantities, and special handling requirements.

2. Scheduling and Cutoff
   - For same-day shipment requests, ensure pick, pack and staging requests are received by 15:00 (3:00 PM) local time at Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Joliet.
   - Confirm carrier appointment times and dock assignments.

3. Pick and Pack Execution
   - Pick orders in sequence per the manifest and pick list.
   - Pack to company packaging standards and apply required labeling and documentation.

4. Staging and Load Preparation
   - Move packed units to outbound staging area.
   - Verify staging against manifest and release units only after documentation is complete.

5. Carrier Arrival and Loading
   - Direct carrier to assigned dock.
   - Provide carrier with required paperwork; accept carrier load inspection and sign-off when loading is complete.

6. Dispatch and Documentation
   - Update WMS and notify customer of shipment completion.
   - Archive all pick, pack, and load documentation per record retention policy.

7. Billing and Accessorials
   - Forward applicable accessorials and minimum charges to Finance for billing.

## 6. Loading / Free Time
This SOP provides carriers 45 minutes of free time for loading at Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Joliet. Dock managers will monitor loading start and completion times. Any time beyond the allowed loading free time is subject to detention charges as specified in Section 7.

When traffic, weather, or unforeseen events affect carrier arrival windows, Operations will communicate expected delays to carriers and Sales.

## 7. Accessorial Charges
### 7.1 Detention
This SOP charges detention at $85.00 per hour after outbound free time at Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Joliet. Detention invoicing is calculated from the end of the allowed loading period until the carrier completes loading and departs the facility.

### 7.2 TONU
This SOP applies a TONU fee of $200.00 unless the Customer provides written notice at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled pickup. This TONU policy applies to customers served from Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Joliet, including Lakeshore Retail Group, and will be billed to the customer when a truck is tendered but the shipment is not presented for loading.

### 7.3 Minimum Charge
This SOP sets a minimum outbound charge of $45.00 per shipment at Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Joliet. Finance will apply the minimum outbound charge where line-haul or other charges would otherwise fall below the minimum.

## 8. Cutoff Times / Same-Day Shipping
This SOP requires pick, pack and staging requests for same-day shipment to be received by 15:00 (3:00 PM) local time at Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Joliet. Requests received after that cutoff will be processed on the next operational cycle, unless Operations authorizes an exception.

## 9. Escalation / Exceptions
Orders not picked within 12 hours of scheduled pick time will be escalated to Operations. The escalation process:
1. First notification: warehouse lead alerts Operations manager.
2. Second notification: Operations manager notifies Sales & Commercial for customer communication.
3. Final notification: If unresolved, Operations escalates to senior operations leadership for resolution.

Exceptions to standard procedure (including but not limited to customer special handling, carrier disputes, or equipment shortages) shall be documented and submitted to Operations for approval. When an exception is tied to an accessorial (for example TONU), the exception will be recorded in the same entry as the accessorial authorization.

## 10. Records and KPIs
All outbound documentation including pick tickets, pack lists, staging manifests, carrier load confirmations, and accessorial invoices must be retained in accordance with Ridgeline Freight, LLC record retention policy. Key performance indicators to be monitored monthly include:
- On-time pick completion (percent of picks completed per scheduled pick window).
- Load accuracy (percent of loads matched to manifest).
- Accessorial incidence (count and type).
- Carrier turnaround (loading time against free time and detention).

The following simple checklist supports outbound readiness and verification:

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Pick verified | Yes |
| Pack complete | Yes |
| Staging confirmed | Yes |
| Carrier appointment confirmed | Yes |
| Documentation attached | Yes |

Operations will produce periodic reports for management and account reconciliation.

## 11. Force Majeure
Ridgeline Freight, LLC will not be liable for delays or failures caused by events beyond reasonable control, including natural disasters, acts of government, labor disputes, or carrier breakdowns. Parties will use reasonable efforts to mitigate the impact of such events and will exchange timely communications.

## 12. Governing Law and Venue
This SOP is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois. Venue for any dispute arising from or relating to the application of this SOP shall be in Cook County, Illinois.

## 13. Notices
All notices under this SOP must be in writing and delivered to the appropriate Ridgeline Freight, LLC department address on record. Operational notices affecting pick, pack, and staging must be delivered to Operations in sufficient time to act, consistent with stated cutoff and escalation provisions.
