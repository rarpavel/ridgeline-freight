# SOP — Hazardous Materials Handling

## 1. Purpose
This Standard Operating Procedure establishes Ridgeline Freight, LLC controls and requirements for the receipt, handling, storage, transport and documentation of hazardous materials. The procedure protects people, property and environment while maintaining compliance with Ridgeline Freight, LLC commercial obligations and internal risk controls.

Ridgeline Freight, LLC headquarters: 1432 Quarrystone Avenue, Chicago, IL 60632.

## 2. Scope
This SOP applies to all Ridgeline Freight, LLC employees, agents, contractors, and carrier partners engaged in the movement or custody of hazardous materials on behalf of customers. It covers acceptance, inspection, placarding, temperature control for refrigerated hazmat, incident reporting, records retention and dispute handling at Ridgeline Freight facilities, including Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Twin Cities, 402 Meridian Park Drive, Minneapolis, MN 55411, docks 12.

This SOP supplements customer-specific terms and carrier agreements where applicable.

## 3. Definitions
- Hazardous materials: Substances requiring specialized handling, documentation and placarding as defined by applicable law and by customer instruction.
- Carrier partner: A third-party transportation provider engaged by Ridgeline Freight, LLC, including but not limited to Juniper Bend Logistics and Granite Fork Intermodal.
- Custody: Physical control of freight by Ridgeline Freight, LLC or by a carrier acting under Ridgeline Freight, LLC direction.
- OSD: Over, short or damaged condition discovered at delivery or receipt.
- Complete hazmat paperwork: All shipping documents, emergency response information and declarations required to accompany a hazardous shipment.

## 5. Acceptance / Notice Requirements
SOP — Hazardous Materials Handling requires hazardous shipments to be approved and documented at least 72 hours before scheduled pickup. Customers must provide complete shipment details and any special handling instructions within that timeframe to permit acceptance review and routing.

Customer-specific handling instructions that affect acceptance must be supplied before approval. Where a customer has standing arrangements with Ridgeline Freight, LLC, those arrangements do not negate the requirement that hazardous shipments be approved and documented at least 72 hours before scheduled pickup.

## 6. Fees / Hazmat Handling
Ridgeline Freight, LLC charges a hazmat handling fee of $200.00 per shipment, except for shipments moved by Juniper Bend Logistics. The fee covers processing, additional documentation, specialized handling, and record retention obligations incurred while hazardous goods are in Ridgeline custody.

Fee application:
- The fee is assessed at shipment acceptance unless alternative commercial terms are documented in a separate customer agreement.
- Exceptions and credits will be handled through commercial approval processes and must be documented in writing.

## 7. Transport / Documentation
Placarding and complete hazmat paperwork required during transport. All hazardous shipments must be accompanied by the required paperwork while in transit and must display appropriate placards or markings on the vehicle or container.

Carrier responsibilities:
- Carrier partners engaged by Ridgeline Freight, LLC must ensure placarding and that complete hazmat paperwork accompany the load at all times.
- Carrier audits will include verification that placarding and paperwork were present at pickup and maintained in transit.

Example carrier engagement: Granite Fork Intermodal is a carrier partner and is required to comply with the above documentation and placarding obligations when handling hazardous shipments on Ridgeline Freight, LLC business.

## 8. Temperature Control / Hazmat Reefer
Reefer hazmat shipments under Ridgeline custody must be maintained at 36°F to 46°F. This range applies to refrigerated hazardous shipments stored or staged at Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Twin Cities and to reefer equipment managed by Ridgeline during custody.

Temperature monitoring and controls:
- Refrigerated units must be prequalified and validated to maintain 36°F to 46°F.
- Temperature logs must accompany each reefer hazmat shipment record while in Ridgeline custody.

## 9. OSD / Reporting for Hazmat
Any over, short or damaged (OSD) condition for a hazmat shipment must be reported within 48 hours of delivery for acceptance of investigation. Timely reporting enables preservation of evidence, assignment of responsibility, and initiation of remediation plans.

OSD reporting process:
1. Recipient documents the condition and notifies Ridgeline operations.
2. Ridgeline logs the OSD and issues an investigation ticket.
3. If required, samples or photographs are secured under controlled conditions to support investigation.

Reports submitted after the 48 hours threshold may be considered untimely and may limit investigatory remedies.

## 10. Procedures
10.1 General acceptance procedure
1. Receive shipment notice and determine whether the shipment contains hazardous materials.
2. Verify that acceptance documentation is provided and that the shipment was approved and documented at least 72 hours before scheduled pickup.
3. Confirm billing and fee applicability, applying the hazmat handling fee of $200.00 per shipment unless the shipment is moved by Juniper Bend Logistics.

10.2 Receipt and inspection
1. At arrival, verify driver paperwork and placarding.
2. Inspect packaging integrity and document any visible damage.
3. For refrigerated hazmat, check temperature controls and confirm maintenance of 36°F to 46°F prior to unloading.

10.3 Storage and staging
1. Place hazardous materials in designated secure areas with appropriate segregation and ventilation.
2. Maintain reefer hazmat within 36°F to 46°F when stored under Ridgeline custody.
3. Control access to hazardous storage areas; only trained personnel may enter.

10.4 Dispatch and transport
1. Verify placarding and complete hazmat paperwork required during transport before release to carrier.
2. Confirm carrier acceptance of special handling requirements and temperature control obligations.
3. Document chain of custody through handover records.

10.5 Incident response
1. For spills, exposures or other emergencies, follow the site emergency response plan and notify operations leadership.
2. Ensure regulatory reporting where applicable and preserve evidence for internal investigation.
3. Submit incident and corrective action reports to commercial and operations leadership.

## 11. Exceptions and Escalation
- Fee exception: Ridgeline Freight, LLC charges a hazmat handling fee of $200.00 per shipment, except for shipments moved by Juniper Bend Logistics. This exception is applied at billing and must be documented in the shipment record.
- Operational exceptions to temperature control, placarding or documentation must be escalated to the Chief Operating Officer, Denise Harrow, for written approval.
- Customer commercial exceptions must be escalated to the VP, Sales & Commercial, Liam Granger, and documented.

Escalation steps:
1. Notify immediate supervisor of the exception.
2. Escalate to Operations Supervisor and Warehouse Manager for local resolution.
3. If unresolved, escalate to Chief Operating Officer for final operational determination.

## 12. Records and Key Performance Indicators
Records to retain:
- Acceptance approvals and documentation demonstrating hazardous shipments were approved and documented at least 72 hours before scheduled pickup.
- Temperature logs for reefer hazmat showing maintenance of 36°F to 46°F while in custody.
- OSD reports and investigation documentation initiated within 48 hours of delivery.
- Billing records showing application of the hazmat handling fee of $200.00 per shipment and any documented exceptions for carriers such as Juniper Bend Logistics.
- Transport records demonstrating that placarding and complete hazmat paperwork required during transport accompanied the load.

Key performance indicators (qualitative and operational):
- Compliance with acceptance timing.
- Incidence of OSD reported within 48 hours.
- Percentage of reefer hazmat maintained within 36°F to 46°F while in custody (measurement methodology to be defined in operational metrics).
