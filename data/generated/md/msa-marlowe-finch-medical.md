# Master Service Agreement — Marlowe & Finch Medical Supply

## 1. Parties
The Master Service Agreement — Marlowe & Finch Medical Supply is between Ridgeline Freight, LLC and Marlowe & Finch Medical Supply.

Ridgeline Freight, LLC (the "Carrier" or "Provider")
- Headquarters: 1432 Quarrystone Avenue, Chicago, IL 60632
- Officers: Chief Executive Officer — Marian Voss; Chief Financial Officer — Owen Carlisle; Chief Operating Officer — Denise Harrow; VP, Sales & Commercial — Liam Granger

Marlowe & Finch Medical Supply (the "Customer")
- Location: Indianapolis, IN
- Industry: Medical Supply
- Commodity: medical devices and disposables
- Volume band: 1k-5k

Each of Ridgeline Freight, LLC and Marlowe & Finch Medical Supply is a “Party” and together the “Parties.”

## 2. Agreement Date and Term
The effective date of the Master Service Agreement — Marlowe & Finch Medical Supply is 03/15/2026.

This Agreement will remain in effect until terminated in accordance with Section 9. The Parties may agree in writing to extend or amend the term.

## 3. Scope of Services and Service Levels
3.1 Scope
Carrier shall provide transportation and related logistics services for Customer’s shipments of medical devices and disposables as requested by Customer and accepted by Carrier. Services include pickup, transportation, routing, delivery, and other agreed logistics activities necessary to move Customer freight.

3.2 Service Levels
Carrier will use commercially reasonable efforts to meet agreed pickup and delivery instructions. Service levels, performance metrics, and any specific service commitments referenced by Customer and Carrier will be documented in executed shipment orders, statements of work, or separate service exhibits incorporated into this Agreement by reference.

3.3 Special Handling
Carrier will employ handling and packaging practices appropriate for medical devices and disposables. Where temperature control, special equipment, or certifications are required, Carrier and Customer will document requirements in writing prior to Carrier’s acceptance of the shipment.

## 4. Rates, Charges and Accessorials
4.1 Rate Reference
All transportation rates and charges for services under this Agreement shall be as set forth in Carrier’s applicable rate sheet, tariff, or executed pricing addendum, each of which is incorporated by reference.

4.2 Accessorial Charges
Accessorial charges for services such as detention, layover, lift gate, special handling, or other additional services will be applied in accordance with Carrier’s published accessorial schedules or as otherwise agreed in writing.

4.3 Taxes and Surcharges
Customer is responsible for any applicable taxes, duties, or governmental charges associated with the services, and for any lawful fuel or regulatory surcharges incorporated into Carrier’s pricing.

## 5. Payment Terms and Invoicing
5.1 Payment Terms
Customer shall pay Carrier in accordance with Carrier’s invoices under the payment terms: Net 15. Invoices will be issued in a timely manner and will reference the relevant shipment details and charges.

5.2 Disputed Charges
If Customer disputes an invoice, Customer shall notify Carrier promptly and provide supporting information. The Parties shall cooperate to resolve disputes. Undisputed portions of any invoice shall be paid in accordance with the payment terms.

5.3 Method of Payment
Payments shall be made by the method specified by Carrier. Carrier may suspend services for nonpayment in accordance with the Agreement and applicable law.

## 6. Late Delivery Remedies and Credits
6.1 Remedies
If a shipment is delivered late in a manner that constitutes a breach of an agreed service requirement, Customer’s remedies will be limited to service credits or other remedies expressly agreed in writing for that shipment.

6.2 Late Delivery Fee
For late deliveries where a flat fee remedy is applicable, the Parties agree that the fee is $250.00 per late delivery, subject to any applicable offsets or agreed credits for excusable delay.

6.3 Procedure for Claiming Remedies
Customer must notify Carrier of any service failure and request remedies within a commercially reasonable time after delivery, providing documentation that supports the claim. Carrier will investigate and respond in a timely manner.

## 7. Claims, Cargo Liability and Insurance
7.1 Claims Procedure
All claims for loss, damage, or shortage must be presented to Carrier in writing promptly and in accordance with Carrier’s claims procedures. Claims must include shipment identification and supporting documentation.

7.2 Liability Cap
Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement or by law, Carrier’s liability for loss of, or damage to, Customer’s cargo shall be limited to $500,000.00 per shipment.

7.3 Exceptions to Liability Limits
Standard liability limits do not apply to reefer shipments; reefer shipments follow a separate higher liability schedule.

7.4 Insurance Requirements
Carrier will maintain commercial liability and cargo insurance as required by applicable law and commercially reasonable standards for the services provided. Upon request, Carrier will provide certificates of insurance evidencing coverage.

## 8. Indemnification and Limitation of Liability
8.1 Carrier Indemnity
Carrier shall defend, indemnify and hold Customer harmless from third-party claims arising out of Carrier’s negligent acts or omissions in the performance of transportation services, subject to the liability limits set forth in this Agreement.

8.2 Customer Indemnity
Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold Carrier harmless from third-party claims arising from Customer’s instructions, the nature or packaging of the cargo, or any breach by Customer of this Agreement.

8.3 Limitation
Except for willful misconduct or indemnities expressly stated to survive, neither Party shall be liable to the other for indirect, consequential, special, or punitive damages arising out of this Agreement.

## 9. Termination
9.1 Termination for Convenience
Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon 15 days prior written notice to the other Party.

9.2 Termination for Cause
A Party may terminate for material breach if the other Party fails to cure a material breach within a commercially reasonable time after written notice specifying the breach.

9.3 Effect of Termination
Termination does not relieve either Party of obligations accrued prior to termination, including payment of charges for services rendered and resolution of outstanding claims.

## 10. Confidentiality and Data Protection
10.1 Confidential Information
Each Party shall maintain in confidence and not disclose Confidential Information of the other Party, except as required by law or as necessary to perform obligations under this Agreement. Confidential Information includes pricing, commercial terms, shipment data, and any non-public business information.

10.2 Use of Data
Carrier may use shipment and routing data solely to provide services under this Agreement and to improve operational performance. Customer grants Carrier a limited license to use such data for the performance of services and for internal analytics consistent with industry practice.

10.3 Return or Destruction
Upon termination of this Agreement, each Party will return or destroy Confidential Information of the other Party, subject to retention for legal or compliance purposes.

## 11. Governing Law and Venue
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois; venue in Cook County, Illinois. The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in Cook County, Illinois for disputes arising under this Agreement.

## 12. Notices, Miscellaneous Provisions and Signatures
12.1 Notices
All notices required under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered to the Parties’ designated business contacts. Notices may be delivered by hand, courier, or other commercially reasonable means.

12.2 Assignment
Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Carrier may assign to affiliates or in connection with a sale of substantially all of its assets.

12.3 Force Majeure
Neither Party will be liable for delays or failures in performance due to causes beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, labor disputes, governmental actions, or other force majeure events. The affected Party shall provide prompt notice and use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate the effects.

12.4 Entire Agreement and Amendments
This Agreement, together with referenced rate sheets, exhibits and executed orders, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter and supersedes prior negotiations and agreements. Amendments must be in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

12.5 Severability
If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions will remain in full force and effect.

12.6 Counterparts
This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which will be deemed an original and together constitute one instrument.
