# Carrier Agreement — Brightwater Carriers LLC

## 1. Parties

### 1.1 Agreement Parties
This Carrier Agreement is between Ridgeline Freight, LLC and Brightwater Carriers LLC. Ridgeline Freight, LLC will act in the capacities of Broker and Shipper where applicable; Brightwater Carriers LLC will act as Carrier.

### 1.2 Ridgeline Contact Information
Ridgeline Freight, LLC
1432 Quarrystone Avenue
Chicago, IL 60632

### 1.3 Carrier Identifiers
Carrier legal name: Brightwater Carriers LLC  
State of registration: WI  
Reported fleet size band: 150-300  
Equipment types represented by Carrier: dry van, reefer, flatbed

## 2. Agreement Date / Term

### 2.1 Effective Date
This Agreement is effective as of 02/01/2026 (the "Effective Date").

### 2.2 Term
This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until terminated in accordance with Section 11.

## 3. Definitions

### 3.1 Defined Terms
Unless the context requires otherwise, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:
- "Broker" or "Shipper" means Ridgeline Freight, LLC.
- "Carrier" means Brightwater Carriers LLC.
- "Load" means a consignment of freight tendered under a specific load confirmation or bill of lading.
- "Hazmat shipments" means any shipment that is subject to applicable hazardous materials laws or regulations.

## 4. Scope of Services

### 4.1 Transportation Services
Carrier will transport freight as directed by Broker pursuant to individual load confirmations and bills of lading. Carrier will provide equipment and personnel required to perform the carriage in a timely, safe, and commercially reasonable manner consistent with industry practice and applicable law.

### 4.2 Rate Confirmations
Each shipment will be governed by a written load confirmation. In the event of any conflict between a load confirmation and this Agreement, the terms of the load confirmation will govern for that specific shipment, provided they do not conflict with applicable law.

## 5. Carrier Responsibilities

### 5.1 Compliance and Safety
Carrier warrants that it is properly authorized and qualified to provide transportation services, that it will at all times comply with applicable laws, and that its drivers and equipment meet industry safety standards. Carrier will maintain and produce on request all permits, licenses, and records required by law.

### 5.2 Personnel and Equipment
Carrier will ensure that drivers assigned to loads are competent, properly licensed, and trained for the cargo being carried. Carrier will supply and maintain equipment appropriate to the freight type, including but not limited to dry van, reefer, and flatbed equipment matched to shipment requirements.

### 5.3 Subcontracting
Carrier may subcontract carriage only in accordance with applicable law and only if Carrier remains fully liable to Broker for the acts and omissions of any subcontractor used.

## 6. Equipment and Compliance

### 6.1 Equipment Condition
Carrier will ensure all equipment is maintained, safe, and suitable for the cargo. Carrier is responsible for compliance with temperature control, securement, and loading requirements for the cargo type.

### 6.2 Regulatory Compliance
Carrier will comply with regulatory inspections and will notify Broker of any events that materially affect Carrier's authority or ability to perform under this Agreement.

## 7. Fees & Accessorials

### 7.1 General
Carrier's compensation for movements will be as set forth in individual load confirmations. Accessorial charges will be established in writing or by prior agreement.

### 7.2 Detention and Free Time
Detention will be charged at $50.00 per hour after 2 hours free time at pickup or delivery. Free time at pickup and delivery under the Brightwater Carrier Agreement is 2 hours.

### 7.3 Additional Accessorials
Unless otherwise agreed in writing, Carrier will invoice for accessorials that were preapproved by Broker. Invoices for accessorials must reference the applicable load confirmation.

## 8. Insurance & Liability

### 8.1 Insurance Requirements
Carrier will maintain insurance customary for the transportation services performed, meeting applicable statutory requirements and sufficient to protect Broker's and Carrier's interests. Carrier will provide certificates of insurance upon Broker's reasonable request.

### 8.2 Cargo Liability
Under this Agreement, cargo liability is limited to $150,000.00 per occurrence, except for hazmat shipments. Carrier will remain responsible for loss or damage to cargo to the extent permitted by law.

### 8.3 Other Liability
Carrier will be liable for loss, damage, or delay resulting from Carrier's negligence, willful misconduct, or failure to comply with applicable laws, subject to the limitations stated in this Agreement.

## 9. Payment Terms

### 9.1 Standard Payment
Payment for services under the Brightwater Carrier Agreement is due Net 30. Carrier will submit properly itemized invoices referencing the Broker's load confirmation number and bill of lading. Broker will remit payment in accordance with the Net 30 term.

### 9.2 Late Payment
Late payments will incur a fee of 1.5% per month on the outstanding balance, calculated from the date payment is due until paid in full.

### 9.3 Quick-Pay Option
Broker may, at its discretion and upon Carrier's election, offer an early payment option. Any early payment terms, fees, or discounts must be agreed in writing and reflected in the applicable load confirmation or written amendment to this Agreement.

## 10. Non-Solicitation, Confidentiality, and Data

### 10.1 Non-Solicitation
During the term of this Agreement and for a reasonable period following termination, Carrier will not directly solicit business from Broker's customers in a manner intended to circumvent Broker. Parties will work in good faith to resolve any commercial disputes.

### 10.2 Confidentiality
Each party will protect confidential business information received from the other and will use such information only for purposes of performing under this Agreement.

### 10.3 Records and Audits
Carrier will maintain records relating to services rendered and will, upon reasonable request, permit Broker to audit relevant records for the purpose of verifying compliance.

## 11. Term and Termination

### 11.1 Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience with 30 days' written notice.

### 11.2 Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate immediately for material breach that is not cured within a reasonable period after written notice, or for insolvency or revocation of required authorities.

### 11.3 Effect of Termination
Termination will not affect rights and obligations accrued prior to termination, including payment obligations for services rendered before termination.

## 12. Force Majeure

Neither party will be liable for delays or failures in performance caused by events beyond the reasonable control of the affected party, provided the affected party gives prompt notice and makes commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

## 13. Notices

Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered to the party's principal place of business or other address provided in writing. Notices will be effective upon receipt.

## 14. Governing Law, Venue, and Miscellaneous

### 14.1 Governing Law and Venue
This Agreement will be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois. The parties submit to exclusive venue in Cook County, Illinois for disputes arising out of or related to this Agreement, to the extent permitted by law.

### 14.2 Independent Contractor
Carrier is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, or joint venture relationship.

### 14.3 Entire Agreement
This Agreement, together with any load confirmations and written amendments, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes prior discussions and understandings.

### 14.4 Severability
If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remainder of the Agreement will remain in full force and effect.
