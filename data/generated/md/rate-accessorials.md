# Accessorial Fee Schedule (2026)

Effective date: 04/01/2026

This Accessorial Fee Schedule (2026) applies to all shipments moved by Ridgeline Freight, LLC and governs accessorial charges applicable to customers and warehouse partners.

## Accessorial Charges

| Service | Unit | Charge | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Detention | Per hour after free time | Free time: 1 hour; charge thereafter: $60.00 per hour | Free detention time applies at both pickup and delivery. |
| Layover | Per day | $350.00 | Applies when driver is held beyond scheduled appointment resulting in a full-day delay. |
| TONU (Truck Ordered Not Used) | Per occurrence | $500.00 | Charged when a truck is ordered and subsequently cancelled without sufficient notice. |
| Lumper | Per stop | $125.00 | Standard lumper fee is $125.00 per stop, except where the third-party receipt documents a different actual cost. |
| Liftgate | Per occurrence | $75.00 | Charged when liftgate service is required for pickup or delivery. |
| Inside Delivery | Per pallet | $40.00 | Applies to placement inside the delivery location beyond standard curbside delivery. |
| Residential Delivery | Per stop | $50.00 | Applies to deliveries to residential addresses that require additional handling or coordination. |
| Hazmat Handling | Per shipment | $200.00 | Hazardous materials handling fee is $200.00 per shipment unless the Customer provides complete hazmat paperwork at least 24 hours before pickup. |
| Redelivery | Per occurrence | $85.00 | Charged when a subsequent delivery attempt is required due to refusal, inability to deliver, or incorrect consignee availability. |
| Storage | Per pallet per day | $12.50 | Storage charges begin after the free unloading window and are billed per pallet per day. |

## Notes

1. Payment for accessorial charges follows Net 15.
