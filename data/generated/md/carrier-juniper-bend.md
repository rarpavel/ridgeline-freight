# Carrier Agreement — Juniper Bend Logistics

## 1. Parties
This Carrier Agreement is between Ridgeline Freight, LLC and Juniper Bend Logistics. Ridgeline Freight, LLC is referred to in this Agreement as "Broker" or "Shipper." Juniper Bend Logistics is referred to in this Agreement as "Carrier."

Ridgeline Freight, LLC headquarters:
1432 Quarrystone Avenue
Chicago, IL 60632

Juniper Bend Logistics is registered in Ohio, operates a fleet of 200-500 vehicles, and runs dry van, reefer, and container chassis equipment.

## 2. Agreement Date / Term
### 2.1 Effective Date
This Agreement shall be effective as of 03/01/2026 (the "Effective Date").

### 2.2 Term
The Agreement shall continue in effect until terminated in accordance with the termination provisions set forth in Section 9.

## 3. Definitions and Scope of Services
### 3.1 Definitions
For purposes of this Agreement:
- "Services" means transportation, pickup, delivery, and related services performed by Carrier pursuant to load offers tendered by Broker.
- "Cargo" means goods tendered by Shipper or Broker for transport under this Agreement.
- "Equipment" means the vehicle types and ancillary equipment used by Carrier to perform Services, including dry van, reefer, container chassis.

### 3.2 Scope
Carrier shall provide transportation services as an independent contractor. Carrier accepts tendered loads subject to the terms of this Agreement and applicable law. Broker may tender Loads to Carrier from time to time; acceptance of any tendered Load creates a separate transport obligation governed by the applicable bill of lading, rate confirmation, or load confirmation and by this Agreement.

## 4. Carrier Obligations; Compliance and Safety Representations
### 4.1 Operating Authority and Compliance
Carrier represents and warrants that it holds and will maintain all licenses, permits, operating authority, and registrations required to perform the Services, and that Carrier and its drivers will comply with applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations while performing Services.

### 4.2 Safety, Security and Drivers
Carrier shall ensure that drivers are properly qualified, trained, and competent. Carrier shall comply with Broker’s reasonable safety and security requirements communicated in writing. Carrier shall maintain chain of custody and shipment security measures appropriate to the cargo.

### 4.3 Equipment Standards
Carrier shall provide and maintain Equipment in good working order and suitable for the cargo type, including but not limited to dry van, reefer, container chassis, as applicable to the load.

## 5. Insurance, Liability and Cargo Claims
### 5.1 Insurance Maintenance
Carrier shall, at its expense, procure and maintain insurance coverage as required by law and as reasonably requested by Broker. Carrier shall provide certificates of insurance upon Broker’s request and shall notify Broker promptly of any material change, cancellation, or nonrenewal of coverage.

### 5.2 Cargo Liability
Carrier’s cargo liability for shipments transported under this Agreement is limited to $250,000.00 per occurrence, except for shipments transported on container chassis.

### 5.3 Claims Handling
Carrier shall promptly notify Broker of any loss, damage, shortage, or other cargo claim and shall cooperate in the investigation and resolution of claims. Carrier shall preserve and make available records, bills of lading, delivery receipts, inspections, and other documentation reasonably necessary to process any claim.

## 6. Fees, Accessorials and Detention
### 6.1 Rates and Confirmations
Rates for each load shall be agreed in writing by the parties via rate confirmation, bill of lading, or electronic load confirmation. Unless otherwise set forth in a specific load confirmation, the rate confirmed for a load governs that movement.

### 6.2 Free Time; Detention
Free time at pickup and delivery under this Agreement is 90 minutes. Detention is charged to the party responsible for detention in accordance with the applicable load confirmation; when charged to Broker, detention will be assessed at $60.00 per hour after 90 minutes free time. Carrier shall record arrival and departure times on applicable documents.

### 6.3 Other Accessorials
Any other accessorials must be agreed in advance in writing. Carrier shall not perform additional work that would result in additional charges for which Carrier intends to invoice Broker without prior written authorization from Broker.

## 7. Payment Terms and Quick-Pay Option
### 7.1 Invoicing
Carrier shall submit proper and complete invoices in the form and manner required by Broker and shall include supporting documentation reasonably requested by Broker. Invoices shall reference the load confirmation number or bill of lading number.

### 7.2 Payment Terms
Payment for services under the Juniper Bend Carrier Agreement is due Net 15. Late payments incur a late fee of 1.0% per month on the outstanding balance until paid. Carrier may charge the late fee only to the extent permitted by law.

### 7.3 Quick-Pay Option
Broker may, at its discretion and subject to mutual written agreement for a particular load or series of loads, offer a quick-pay option. Any quick-pay option, including processing procedures and the cost to Carrier, shall be set forth in a separate written agreement or load confirmation.

## 8. Non-Solicitation and Confidentiality
### 8.1 Non-Solicitation
During the term of this Agreement and for a period after termination as provided in the affiliated load confirmations, Carrier shall not directly solicit loads from Broker’s customers for carriage without Broker’s prior written consent. For avoidance of doubt, this is a contractual commitment between the parties.

### 8.2 Confidentiality
Carrier shall treat as confidential all nonpublic business information received from Broker and shall not disclose such information except as required by law or with Broker’s prior written consent. Confidential information includes rates, route plans, customer lists, and operational instructions furnished by Broker.

## 9. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience with 60 days' written notice. Either party may terminate immediately upon material breach by the other party that remains uncured after reasonable written notice when immediate termination is authorized by law or when continued performance would expose the terminating party to undue legal risk.

## 10. Indemnification; Limitation of Liability; Force Majeure
### 10.1 Indemnification
Each party shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless the other party from and against liabilities, losses, damages, costs, and expenses arising out of the negligent acts or willful misconduct of the indemnifying party in performing this Agreement, subject to the limitations set forth elsewhere in this Agreement.

### 10.2 Limitation of Liability
Except as expressly provided elsewhere in this Agreement, neither party shall be liable for consequential, incidental, special, punitive, or exemplary damages arising out of or related to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort, or otherwise, except for liabilities arising from gross negligence or willful misconduct.

### 10.3 Force Majeure
Neither party shall be liable for failure or delay in performing obligations under this Agreement to the extent caused by events beyond such party’s reasonable control, including acts of God, labor disputes, governmental actions, and other similar occurrences. Affected party shall provide prompt notice and use reasonable efforts to resume performance.

## 11. Governing Law, Venue and Notices
### 11.1 Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to choice of law principles.

### 11.2 Venue
Any action arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Cook County, Illinois, and each party consents to the jurisdiction of those courts.

### 11.3 Notices
Notices, demands, and other communications shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses of the parties on record. Broker’s address for notices: 1432 Quarrystone Avenue, Chicago, IL 60632. Carrier shall provide its current business address for notices and shall update Broker promptly of any change.

## 12. Miscellaneous and Signatures
### 12.1 Relationship of Parties
Carrier is an independent contractor and not an agent, employee, joint venturer, or partner of Broker. Carrier shall be responsible for withholding and paying all employment taxes and contributions whether for Carrier’s employees or independent drivers.

### 12.2 Assignment
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except that Broker may assign to an affiliate or successor by operation of law with notice to Carrier.

### 12.3 Severability and Waiver
If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remainder of the Agreement shall remain in full force and effect. No waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing.

### 12.4 Entire Agreement
This Agreement, together with any rate confirmations, bills of lading, and written addenda executed by the parties, constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, proposals, or representations relating to the subject matter hereof.
