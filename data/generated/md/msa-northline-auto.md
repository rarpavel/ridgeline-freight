# Master Service Agreement — Northline Auto Parts

## 1. Parties

### 1.1 Identification
This Master Service Agreement — Northline Auto Parts (the "Agreement") is between Ridgeline Freight, LLC, a transportation provider with principal place of business at 1432 Quarrystone Avenue, Chicago, IL 60632 ("Carrier" or "Provider"), and Northline Auto Parts ("Customer").

### 1.2 Roles
The Carrier shall provide transportation and related logistics services to the Customer as set forth in this Agreement. The Customer shall engage the Carrier for the movement, handling, and related services for Customer shipments consistent with the terms below.

## 2. Agreement Date / Term

### 2.1 Effective Date
This Agreement is effective as of 06/01/2026 (the "Effective Date").

### 2.2 Term
The Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until terminated in accordance with the Term and Termination provisions below.

## 3. Scope of Services

### 3.1 Services Provided
Carrier shall provide transportation and logistics services for the movement of aftermarket auto parts as requested by Customer, including pickup, line-haul, delivery, and reasonable ancillary services. Services will be performed in accordance with mutually agreed instructions and applicable law.

### 3.2 Service Levels
Carrier will exercise commercially reasonable efforts to meet delivery instructions and service levels agreed in writing between the parties for specific lanes or shipments. Service level commitments for particular moves shall be set forth in purchase orders, booking confirmations, or rate sheets referenced in Section 5.

## 4. Rates and Billing

### 4.1 Rates
Carrier’s charges for services rendered under this Agreement shall be as set forth in the applicable Rate Sheet, which is incorporated by reference. Rate Sheets applicable to particular lanes, shipments, or periods will be the controlling schedule for charges.

### 4.2 Billing
Carrier will invoice Customer for services and any applicable accessorial charges in accordance with the applicable Rate Sheet and this Agreement. Invoices will detail the services performed and reference the applicable Rate Sheet or written rate confirmation.

## 5. Payment Terms

### 5.1 Standard Terms
Payment terms for Carrier invoices are Net 21. Customer shall pay undisputed amounts in accordance with these payment terms.

### 5.2 Disputes
If Customer disputes any portion of an invoice, Customer shall notify Carrier in writing and the parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly. Payment of undisputed amounts must not be withheld pending resolution of disputes.

## 6. Accessorial Charges; Exceptions

### 6.1 Accessorial Charges
Accessorial charges apply, excluding federal holidays (no supplemental accessorial billing on federal holidays). All accessorials billed will be supported by documentation and applied in accordance with the applicable Rate Sheet.

### 6.2 Adjustments
If any accessorial charge is assessed in error, Carrier will promptly issue a credit upon validation of the error. The parties will cooperate to correct billing errors in a commercially reasonable manner.

## 7. Late Delivery Remedies and Penalties

### 7.1 Remedies for Late Delivery
If a Carrier-caused late delivery results in invoice adjustments, the parties agree that the following remedy applies: Carrier will be subject to a late delivery penalty of 0.5% per day of the affected invoice amount. This remedy is exclusive of punitive damages and does not limit Customer’s right to seek actual consequential damages where permitted by law.

### 7.2 Application
Penalties described in this Section apply only to shipments where Carrier is the primary cause of delay and where the Customer documents the delay and resulting loss in a timely and reasonable manner.

## 8. Claims, Cargo Liability, and Insurance

### 8.1 Claims Procedure
Claims for loss, damage, or shortage shall be presented in writing to Carrier and shall include shipment identification, description of claimed damage or loss, and supporting documentation. The parties shall cooperate in investigations of claims.

### 8.2 Cargo Liability Cap
Carrier’s liability for loss of or damage to cargo under this Agreement is limited to $10,000.00 per shipment, except to the extent a different limit is required by applicable law or agreed in writing for a particular shipment.

### 8.3 Insurance
Carrier shall maintain commercially reasonable liability and cargo insurance appropriate to its operations. Evidence of insurance will be provided to Customer upon request. Maintenance of insurance is for the protection of the parties and does not expand Carrier’s liability beyond contract terms.

## 9. Indemnification

### 9.1 Carrier Indemnity
Carrier shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Customer from and against claims, liabilities, losses, and expenses to the extent caused by Carrier’s negligent act or omission, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement, subject to the liability limits stated herein.

### 9.2 Customer Indemnity
Customer shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Carrier from claims arising from Customer’s instructions, negligent packaging or labeling of cargo, or Customer’s breach of this Agreement.

## 10. Term and Termination

### 10.1 Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate this Agreement with 90 days' prior written notice to the other party.

### 10.2 Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breaching party fails to cure the breach within a reasonable period after written notice of breach. Termination for cause will be effective as provided in the written termination notice.

### 10.3 Effect of Termination
Upon termination, Customer shall pay Carrier for services performed and valid charges incurred through the effective date of termination, subject to the dispute resolution provisions of this Agreement.

## 11. Confidentiality

### 11.1 Confidential Information
Each party shall keep confidential and not disclose to third parties nonpublic information received from the other party that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, and shall use such information only for performance under this Agreement.

### 11.2 Exclusions
Confidential information does not include information that is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement, is independently developed without use of the other party’s confidential information, or is required to be disclosed by law.

## 12. Force Majeure

Neither party shall be liable for delays or failures in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, natural disasters, labor disturbances, governmental actions, or other causes that make performance impracticable or impossible. The affected party shall provide prompt notice of a force majeure event and resume performance as soon as reasonably practicable.

## 13. Miscellaneous Provisions

### 13.1 Independent Contractor
Carrier performs services as an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, agency, or joint venture relationship between the parties.

### 13.2 Assignment
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party’s prior written consent, except that Carrier may subcontract performance to carriers or service providers consistent with industry practice and this Agreement.

### 13.3 Entire Agreement
This Agreement, together with referenced Rate Sheets and written service confirmations, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning its subject matter and supersedes prior proposals, communications, and agreements.

### 13.4 Amendments
Amendments to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

### 13.5 Severability
If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

## 14. Governing Law and Venue

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois; venue in Cook County, Illinois. The parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Cook County, Illinois for resolution of disputes arising under this Agreement, except as otherwise agreed in writing.

## 15. Notices

All notices required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the parties at their principal business addresses. For Carrier: Ridgeline Freight, LLC, 1432 Quarrystone Avenue, Chicago, IL 60632. For Customer: notices shall be directed to the address on file with Carrier or otherwise provided in writing by Customer.
