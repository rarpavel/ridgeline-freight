# Employee Handbook — Code of Conduct and At-Will Employment

## 1. Purpose
This Employee Handbook establishes standards of conduct and workplace expectations for employees of Ridgeline Freight, LLC. The purpose is to describe the Company’s core principles, employee responsibilities, reporting procedures, disciplinary approach, and legal framework that guide day-to-day operations. This Handbook is intended to promote a professional, safe, and compliant workplace while preserving the flexibility set by Company policy.

## 2. Scope and Eligibility
### 2.1 Applicability
This Handbook applies to all employees of Ridgeline Freight, LLC, including full-time, part-time, temporary, and contract staff while working on Company premises, on Company business, or when representing the Company.

### 2.2 Relationship to Other Policies
This Handbook complements other policies, agreements, and notices issued by the Company. Where a written employment agreement expressly modifies a subject addressed in this Handbook, the terms of that agreement govern to the extent permitted by law.

## 4. Employment Status
Employment with Ridgeline Freight, LLC is based on mutual consent and either the Company or the employee may terminate the relationship at any time, for any lawful reason. Employment is at-will. Nothing in this Handbook is intended to create a contract of employment for a definite period of time or to alter the at-will relationship except in writing signed by an authorized Company officer.

## 5. Code of Conduct — Policy Statement
Ridgeline Freight, LLC expects employees to act with honesty, integrity, and professionalism. Employees must avoid conduct that could damage the Company’s reputation, create a hostile work environment, or interfere with safe and efficient operations. This includes, but is not limited to, compliance with laws, truthful communication, respectful treatment of colleagues, and responsible use of Company resources.

### 5.1 Core Principles
- Act in the best interests of the Company and its stakeholders.
- Maintain professional behavior in all dealings with colleagues, customers, and vendors.
- Protect confidential information and proprietary data.
- Report conduct that may violate Company policy or law.

## 6. Conflicts of Interest
Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict, or appear to conflict, with the interests of the Company. Examples include outside employment that interferes with job duties, financial interests in competitors, and accepting gifts that influence business decisions.

Employees must disclose any actual or potential conflicts of interest within 10 days of becoming aware of them. Disclosures should be made to the employee’s manager or Human Resources as set out in Section 10.

## 7. Confidentiality and Use of Company Property
Employees have a duty to safeguard Company confidential information. Confidential information includes business plans, customer lists, financial data, and any nonpublic operational details. Use Company property and systems for legitimate business purposes only, and follow any applicable access and data handling procedures.

Upon separation from employment, employees must return Company property and comply with post-employment confidentiality obligations described in applicable agreements.

## 8. Resignation and Notice
Employees who voluntarily resign are asked to make the transition as orderly as possible. 14 days' notice requested but not required. When possible, the employee should cooperate in knowledge transfer and return of Company property. The Company may accept resignation immediately or request that the employee remain to assist with transition.

## 9. Discipline and Enforcement
Ridgeline Freight, LLC follows a consistent disciplinary approach to address conduct that violates policy or performance expectations. The Company will consider the nature and severity of the issue, prior conduct, and other relevant factors.

The Company uses Progressive discipline up to and including termination. Depending on circumstances, disciplinary measures may include counseling, written warnings, suspension, reassignment, or termination.

## 10. Reporting, Investigation, and Protections
### 10.1 Reporting Procedures
Employees are expected to report known or suspected violations promptly. Reports may be made to any of the following channels:
1. Direct manager
2. Human Resources
3. Other designated reporting channels specified by the Company

Reports should include relevant facts, names, dates, and any available documentation. The Company will handle reported matters with discretion to the extent practicable.

### 10.2 Investigation Process
1. The Company will acknowledge receipt of a report and assess whether an investigation is warranted.
2. Investigations will be conducted promptly and fairly by qualified personnel.
3. Investigators will gather relevant information, interview involved parties, and review documentation.
4. Investigative findings will determine appropriate corrective action consistent with Company policy.

### 10.3 Non-Retaliation
The Company prohibits retaliation against individuals who report concerns in good faith or participate in investigations. Employees who believe they have suffered retaliation should report it immediately.

## 11. Procedures — Requests, Approvals, and Recordkeeping
Ridgeline Freight, LLC maintains defined procedures for requests and approvals related to conflicts disclosures, leave requests, expense approvals, and access to Company systems. Employees must follow the applicable steps and provide necessary documentation.

General procedure:
1. Submit a written or electronic request to the appropriate approver.
2. Await acknowledgement and, if applicable, follow any interim instructions.
3. Provide additional information promptly if requested by the approver.
4. Retain copies of approvals and related records as required by Company practice.

Managers and approvers are responsible for timely review and consistent application of approvals and recordkeeping practices. Human Resources maintains central records for disclosures, approvals, and personnel actions.

Exceptions to standard procedures may be granted only by an authorized officer and must be documented and retained with the related records.

## 12. Compliance, Acknowledgment, and At-Will Reminder
Compliance with this Handbook and related policies is a condition of employment. Employees are expected to read, understand, and follow these standards. The Company may update or amend policies from time to time; notice of material changes will be provided in accordance with Company practice.

This Handbook is not an employment contract and does not alter the at-will status of employment. For clarity and emphasis: Employment is at-will.

Employees will be asked to sign an acknowledgment confirming receipt and review of this Handbook. Acknowledgment of receipt does not waive any statutory rights employees may hold.
