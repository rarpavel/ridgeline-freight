# Employee Handbook — Attendance and Leaves of Absence

## 1. Purpose
This Attendance and Leaves of Absence policy establishes Ridgeline Freight, LLC’s standards and procedures for employee attendance, sick leave, family and medical leave, jury duty, and related notifications. Ridgeline Freight, LLC operates from its headquarters at 1432 Quarrystone Avenue, Chicago, IL 60632. This document complements the broader employee handbook and applicable employment agreements and is intended to ensure consistency, fairness, and operational continuity.

## 2. Scope and Eligibility
### 2.1 Scope
This policy applies to all employees of Ridgeline Freight, LLC, including full-time and part-time employees, unless an alternative written policy or agreement applies.

### 2.2 Eligibility for Leave
Eligibility for certain leaves under this policy is conditioned on length of continuous service. Employees become eligible for sick leave after 30 days of continuous employment. Other leave types described below include eligibility conditions described in their respective sections.

## 3. Policy Statement
Ridgeline Freight, LLC encourages employees to maintain good attendance and to use leave benefits responsibly. The Company provides job-protected and paid leaves as specified in this policy, and provides unpaid leaves where required by the Company’s obligations and by applicable law. Consistent attendance supports operational reliability and workplace fairness.

## 4. Sick Leave Accrual
Ridgeline Freight, LLC provides paid sick leave accrual for eligible employees as a part of the Company’s attendance and health-supporting benefits.
- Employees accrue 6 days per year of paid sick leave under the Attendance and Leaves of Absence policy.
- Accrued sick leave may be used for the employee’s own illness or medical appointments, and for the care of an immediate family member, subject to applicable eligibility and notification requirements in this policy.
- Accrual and use are subject to the Company’s timekeeping and payroll processes, and employees must follow the procedures in Section 9 to request sick leave.

## 5. Family and Medical Leave
Ridgeline Freight, LLC recognizes serious health conditions and qualifying family events that may require extended leave.
- Employees may take up to 84 days of unpaid family leave in a rolling 12-month period under this policy. Requests for family and medical leave will be handled according to applicable law and this Company policy.
- When leave qualifies under other federal or state statutes, Company procedures for verification, intermittent leave, reinstatement, and health benefit continuation will apply as provided by law and by this policy.
- Employees must provide reasonable documentation to support requests for extended leave. The Company may require medical certification or other verification consistent with applicable legal standards.

## 6. Jury Duty Pay
Ridgeline Freight, LLC encourages employees to fulfill civic duties such as jury service.
- The Company provides jury duty pay up to $500.00 per week while an employee is serving on jury duty.
- Employees must provide proof of service and anticipated duration to their manager and to Human Resources as soon as practicable.

## 7. Absence Notification and Attendance
Reliable attendance and timely notification are critical to operations.
- Employees must notify their manager at least 2 hours before a scheduled shift if they will be unable to report to work.
- When advance notice is not possible, employees must notify their manager as soon as reasonably practicable and provide an explanation for the absence.
- Repeated failures to provide timely notification or pattern absenteeism can lead to disciplinary action up to and including termination, subject to applicable legal protections and other provisions in this policy.

## 8. Attendance Exceptions
The Company recognizes circumstances that warrant exceptions to general attendance rules.
- Absences on federal holidays are excluded from attendance points; the Company will not count Absences on federal holidays are excluded from attendance points for the purposes of attendance point totals.
- Leave taken under protected statutes, approved accommodations, or approved leaves of absence will be treated consistent with law and Company practice and may be excluded from disciplinary attendance calculations.

## 9. Procedures for Requests, Approvals, and Documentation
This section describes the practical steps employees should follow when requesting leave or reporting absences.
1. Reporting an Unplanned Absence:
   - Notify your manager in accordance with Section 7 as soon as you are aware you will be absent.
   - Provide the expected duration of the absence if known and any relevant documentation upon return.
2. Requesting Planned Leave:
   - Submit a leave request to your manager and Human Resources with reasonable advance notice when the absence is foreseeable.
   - Include relevant documentation as requested by Human Resources.
3. Approval Process:
   - Managers will review leave requests and coordinate with Human Resources to determine eligibility, required documentation, and whether alternative coverage is needed.
   - Approval will be communicated to the employee in writing or by the Company’s standard internal communication methods.
4. Returning from Leave:
   - Before returning from approved leave, employees may be required to provide documentation confirming fitness for duty, depending on the nature of the leave and in accordance with applicable law.
5. Recordkeeping:
   - Time and attendance records, leave requests, approvals, and documentation will be maintained in the employee’s personnel file in accordance with Company policy and applicable retention standards.

## 10. Attendance Points, Monitoring, and Discipline
Ridgeline Freight, LLC may monitor attendance patterns to ensure operational reliability.
- The Company uses attendance records and reasonable supervision to track patterns of absenteeism and tardiness.
- Attendance counseling and progressive corrective action may be used when patterns of unexcused absences or failures to follow notification procedures are identified.
- The Company’s disciplinary approach will be applied fairly and consistently, and in compliance with applicable law.

## 11. Compliance, At-Will Employment, and Confidentiality
- Employment with Ridgeline Freight, LLC remains at-will unless otherwise established in a written agreement signed by an authorized officer of the Company. Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment.
- This policy will be administered in compliance with applicable federal, state, and local law.
- Employee health information and leave documentation will be treated as confidential and, to the extent required by law, maintained in a separate confidential medical file.

## 12. Force Majeure and Operational Adjustments
In exceptional circumstances beyond the Company’s control, such as natural disasters or other significant disruptions, the Company may implement temporary operational adjustments to attendance and leave procedures to ensure employee safety and business continuity. Such adjustments will be communicated to employees through official Company channels.

For questions about this policy, employees may contact the following Company officers by title only:
- Chief Executive Officer
- Chief Financial Officer
- Chief Operating Officer
- VP, Sales & Commercial
Human Resources may coordinate inquiries and provide guidance on the application of this policy.
