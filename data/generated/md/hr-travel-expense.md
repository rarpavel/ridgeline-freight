# Employee Handbook — Business Travel and Expense Reimbursement

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets forth Ridgeline Freight, LLC's standards and procedures for business travel and expense reimbursement. It exists to support consistent, fair, and auditable handling of travel and related spending incurred on behalf of Ridgeline Freight, LLC, whose headquarters are at 1432 Quarrystone Avenue, Chicago, IL 60632. The policy balances the company’s need to control costs with the requirement that employees be able to reasonably perform their duties while traveling.

## 2. Scope and Eligibility
This policy applies to all employees of Ridgeline Freight, LLC when travel and expenses are incurred in the performance of company business. Contractors and temporary workers are subject to company direction and only eligible for reimbursement where a written agreement or prior authorization states that expenses will be reimbursed.

## 3. Policy Statement
Ridgeline Freight, LLC reimburses reasonable and necessary business travel expenses that are supported by required documentation and approved according to procedure. Reimbursement is intended to cover expenses incurred for official company business; personal costs are not reimbursable. All reimbursements must comply with internal controls and may be subject to audit. Deviations from the standard policy must be expressly approved as described in the Pre-Approval Exceptions section.

## 4. Definitions
- Business travel: Travel required by the company for work duties away from the employee’s usual work location.
- Authorized traveler: An employee who has obtained required approvals to travel on company business.
- Expense report: The company form or electronic submission combining itemized expenses and required documentation for reimbursement.
- Reasonable: In the judgment of the approving authority, consistent with the company’s expectations for economy and practicality.

## 5. Responsibilities
1. Employees: Ensure expenses are necessary, reasonable, and supported by documentation; submit expense reports in accordance with the Submission Timeline.
2. Managers/Approvers: Review and approve travel authorizations and expense reports, ensuring alignment with business needs and company policy.
3. Finance: Review for policy compliance, process reimbursements, and maintain records for audit and tax purposes.
4. The VP, Sales & Commercial: Holds specific pre-approval authority as detailed in Pre-Approval Exceptions.

## 6. Procedures — Travel Authorization and Booking
1. Obtain managerial approval prior to booking travel when practicable. If pre-approval is not feasible for operational reasons, document the business justification at the time of expense report submission.
2. When reserving travel, select reasonably priced transportation and lodging consistent with business needs.
3. Use corporate tools or preferred suppliers when they are available and practical, to assist with cost control and recordkeeping.
4. If travel is combined with personal time, only the portion attributable to business is reimbursable and must be documented.

## 7. Per Diem
The per diem for meals and incidental expenses is $55.00 per day under the Business Travel and Expense Reimbursement policy. Employees may use the per diem in place of submitting individual meal receipts when the full per diem is applied and no single meal expense is claimed in excess of the per diem. Partial day travel and mixed personal/business days should be handled on a pro rata basis consistent with reasonable allocation.

## 8. Mileage Reimbursement
Reimbursement for use of a personal vehicle on company business is available at the established rate noted in this policy. Mileage for personal vehicle use on business travel is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile. Employees must record origin, destination, purpose, and miles driven; the approver will verify reasonableness. Commuting to and from an employee’s regular work location is not reimbursable.

## 9. Lodging and Accommodations
Employees should select lodging that balances safety, convenience, and reasonableness of cost. The company expects standard single-occupancy accommodations unless a business justification for alternative arrangements is documented and approved. Room upgrades, premium services, and incidental charges for personal entertainment are the responsibility of the employee and are not reimbursable unless pre-approved.

## 10. Receipts and Documentation
Receipts are required for all expenses over $25.00 when submitting travel expense reports. For expenses at or below that threshold, employees may follow the company expense report instructions for itemization without a receipt. All receipts must clearly show date, merchant, and amount. When a receipt is unavailable, employees must provide a written explanation and, where practical, a substitute form of proof such as a bank card statement.

## 11. Submission Timeline
Expense reports and supporting documents must be timely submitted for processing. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of trip completion to be eligible for reimbursement. Late submissions may be reviewed for compliance and may require additional justification; repeated late submissions may affect eligibility for reimbursement.

## 12. Pre-Approval Exceptions
Company policy requires prior approval for certain high-cost or nonstandard items. Overnight lodging above $300.00 requires pre-approval by the VP, Sales & Commercial. Requests for exceptions should include a business justification, expected cost, and proposed approver signature; the approver may impose additional conditions when granting an exception.

## 13. Reimbursement Payment Terms
Reimbursements for approved travel expenses follow a standard payment timeline and processing practice. Reimbursements for approved travel expenses are paid Net 30 from the date of receipt of a complete expense report. A complete expense report contains all required receipts, business purpose, and necessary approvals. Incomplete submissions will be returned for correction and will be processed when resubmitted.

## 14. Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The following types of expenses are ordinarily not reimbursable: personal entertainment, personal care items, fines and penalties, personal travel costs, expenses for family or friends, and expenses that violate company policy or law. If a non-reimbursable expense is charged to a corporate card in error, the employee must reimburse the amount and provide documentation of repayment.

## 15. Corporate Cards and Advances
When corporate cards are issued, they should be used in accordance with this policy. Cardholders remain responsible for proper usage and timely reconciliation. The company may provide cash advances under limited circumstances; advances must be reconciled with receipts and expense reports upon completion of the travel.

## 16. Audits, Recordkeeping, and Fraud
All travel and expense transactions are subject to review and audit. Employees must retain documentation as instructed by Finance and provide additional information upon request. Suspected misuse, false claims, or fraudulent activity must be reported to management or Finance and will be investigated. Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.

## 17. Compliance, Governing Law, and Venue
This policy must be followed by all employees and is subject to internal controls and applicable law. This policy is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois, and any disputes arising under this policy shall be resolved in the courts located in Cook County, Illinois, unless otherwise required by law. Nothing in this document alters the at-will employment relationship where applicable.

## 18. Exceptions and Emergency Circumstances
Managers may grant exceptions to this policy when business need and circumstances justify them. All exceptions should be documented and retained with the expense report. In emergencies or force majeure situations where adherence to the usual procedures is impracticable, employees should act in the company’s best interest and provide a written account of actions taken as soon as reasonably practicable.
