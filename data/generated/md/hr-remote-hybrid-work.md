# Employee Handbook — Remote and Hybrid Work

## 1. Introduction
Ridgeline Freight, LLC is committed to providing flexible work arrangements that support business needs and employee well-being while protecting operational integrity. This policy applies to employees of Ridgeline Freight, LLC, headquartered at 1432 Quarrystone Avenue, Chicago, IL 60632. The policy provides standards and procedures for remote and hybrid work, clarifies roles and responsibilities, and sets expectations for performance, security, and compliance.

## 2. Purpose
The purpose of this handbook is to:
- Define eligibility and permissible remote or hybrid arrangements.
- Describe the application and approval process.
- Explain equipment reimbursement and workplace safety responsibilities.
- Preserve service levels and protect company assets while offering flexibility.

This handbook does not alter the at-will employment relationship between the company and its employees.

## 3. Scope and Eligibility
This policy applies to employees whose job duties can be performed effectively off-site and where business operations permit. Employees become eligible for remote or hybrid work after 180 days of continuous employment. Eligibility is subject to manager assessment and business unit approval. Participation is a privilege and may be modified or revoked based on performance, business need, or other legitimate reasons.

## 4. Eligibility Exceptions
Drivers, warehouse staff, and other on-site roles are not eligible for remote or hybrid work. This exclusion includes personnel assigned to Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Chicago West, 2107 Northwind Terrace, Chicago, IL 60639; Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Joliet, 88 Kestrel Loop, Joliet, IL 60433; and Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Twin Cities, 402 Meridian Park Drive, Minneapolis, MN 55411. The nature of these roles requires physical presence to maintain operational capacity, customer service, and safety.

## 5. Definitions
- Remote work: Performing job duties from an approved off-site location on a recurring or scheduled basis.
- Hybrid work: A mix of on-site and remote work according to an approved schedule.
- Manager: The employee’s direct supervisor or other person with delegated authority to approve work arrangements.
- Company equipment: Devices owned or provided by Ridgeline Freight, LLC that are used to perform work duties.
- Workspace: The area at an employee’s remote location used for work; it must meet basic safety and operational standards.

## 6. Policy Statement
Ridgeline Freight, LLC aims to balance employee flexibility with the needs of our customers and operations. Remote and hybrid schedules are intended to support productivity and continuity while ensuring clear lines of communication, data security, and accountability.

Hybrid work schedules must be established in writing and approved by the manager and human resources. As a guideline for hybrid arrangements, the standard allowance is up to 2 days remote work per week for eligible employees, subject to operational requirements and manager approval.

Remote or hybrid arrangements do not change the employee’s duties, obligations, or reporting relationships. Employees remain responsible for meeting performance and availability expectations regardless of work location.

## 7. Procedures
### 7.1 Requesting Remote or Hybrid Work
1. Employees wishing to work remotely or adopt a hybrid schedule should submit a written request to their manager describing the proposed arrangement, primary remote workspace, and how responsibilities will be met.
2. Managers will assess job duties, team coverage, and operational impact and may consult human resources prior to decision.
3. Approved arrangements must be documented and retained in personnel records.

### 7.2 Approvals and Documentation
1. Managers will notify the employee in writing of approval, conditional approval, or denial.
2. Approvals may include specific conditions such as required on-site days, core hours for availability, and deliverable expectations.
3. Human resources will maintain a record of all active remote and hybrid arrangements.

### 7.3 Equipment and Reimbursement
Ridgeline will reimburse approved remote-work equipment purchases up to a cap of $300.00 per employee. Reimbursement requires prior managerial approval and submission of receipts in accordance with company reimbursement procedures. Company-owned equipment provided for remote work must be used in accordance with company policies and returned upon request.

### 7.4 Schedule Changes and Notice
Employees must provide 7 business days' notice to change an approved hybrid work schedule. Managers and employees should seek to accommodate reasonable requests, but business needs may require denial or adjustment of requested changes.

## 8. Hybrid Work and Scheduling
Managers and employees must agree on a hybrid schedule that meets job requirements and team needs. Schedules should indicate on-site days, remote days, core hours (when the employee is expected to be available), and preferred communication channels. Any deviation from the approved schedule should be communicated to the manager in advance.

Remote work should not impede participation in required meetings, training, or customer interactions. Managers may require an employee to work on-site for particular business needs at the company’s discretion.

## 9. Data Security and Privacy
Employees working remotely must adhere to all company security and privacy policies. Expected safeguards include:
- Using company-approved devices and software for work tasks unless an exception is approved.
- Maintaining secure Wi-Fi connections and protecting devices with strong authentication.
- Reporting any suspected security incident immediately to the IT department.
Employees must not store or transmit sensitive company or customer information outside approved systems.

## 10. Health, Safety, and Ergonomics
Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe and ergonomically appropriate remote workspace. Ridgeline may provide guidance on ergonomic setup but is not responsible for minor home modifications. Company-provided equipment should be used to mitigate ergonomic risk where appropriate. Employees must report any work-related injury that occurs while working remotely in accordance with company reporting procedures.

## 11. Performance, Compliance, and Monitoring
Performance expectations remain consistent regardless of work location. Managers will evaluate employees based on outcomes, quality of work, responsiveness, and adherence to schedule and availability commitments. The company may monitor compliance with remote-work policies and may request periodic status updates or in-person meetings.

Participation in remote or hybrid work does not alter compensation, benefits, or eligibility for programs unless explicitly stated in writing. All employees must comply with company policies, including but not limited to confidentiality, code of conduct, and conflict of interest.

Ridgeline Freight, LLC operates under the laws of the State of Illinois. Any disputes arising from or relating to this policy shall be subject to venue in Cook County, Illinois to the extent permitted by law.

## 12. At-Will Employment and Policy Changes
Employment with Ridgeline Freight, LLC remains at-will and may be terminated by the employee or the company at any time for any lawful reason. Ridgeline reserves the right to modify, suspend, or terminate remote and hybrid work arrangements and this policy for business reasons. Changes will be communicated to impacted employees.
