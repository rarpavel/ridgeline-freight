# Master Service Agreement — Lakeshore Retail Group

## 1. Parties

### 1.1 Identification
This Master Service Agreement — Lakeshore Retail Group is between Ridgeline Freight, LLC (the "Carrier" or "Provider") and Lakeshore Retail Group (the "Customer").

### 1.2 Carrier details
Ridgeline Freight, LLC

Chicago, IL 60632

- Chief Financial Officer: Owen Carlisle
- Chief Operating Officer: Denise Harrow
- VP, Sales & Commercial: Liam Granger

## 2. Agreement Date and Term

### 2.1 Effective date
This agreement is effective as of 04/15/2026.

### 2.2 Term
The agreement continues until terminated in accordance with Section 11. Either party may terminate this agreement with 60 days' prior written notice.

## 3. Definitions

### 3.1 General
Where used in this agreement, the following terms have the meanings given below unless otherwise indicated:
- "Services" means transportation, logistics coordination, and related services provided by the Carrier to the Customer under this agreement.
- "Shipment" means each separate consignment of goods tendered to the Carrier for transport.
- "Accessorials" means additional services or charges not included in the base transportation rate.
- "TONU" means Truck Ordered Not Used.

## 4. Scope of Services and Service Levels

### 4.1 Services provided
The Carrier will provide transportation and related logistics services for general consumer goods consistent with Customer instructions and applicable law. Services shall conform to accepted industry practices and the service levels agreed between the parties.

### 4.2 Service levels
The Carrier will operate to meet reasonable pickup and delivery requirements communicated by the Customer. The parties will cooperate to establish operational procedures and appointment rules applicable to lanes and facilities in scope.

## 5. Rates, Billing, and Payment Terms

### 5.1 Rates
Rates for Services will be set forth in one or more rate sheets, confirmation documents, purchase orders, or electronic rate attachments executed by the parties and incorporated by reference into this agreement. The Carrier's published tariffs are not controlling except as expressly incorporated.

### 5.2 Billing
The Carrier will invoice the Customer in accordance with the applicable rate sheet or confirmation. Each invoice will reference the relevant shipment identifiers and the services performed.

### 5.3 Payment terms
Customer shall pay invoices under this agreement in accordance with the payment terms of Net 45. The Customer's obligation to pay is independent of claims between the parties except as expressly provided in this agreement.

## 6. Accessorial Charges and Exceptions

### 6.1 Accessorials
Accessorial services not included in base rates are chargeable at rates agreed in the applicable rate sheet. Such accessorials include, but are not limited to, detention, layover, reconsignment, and special handling when applicable and provided by the Carrier.

### 6.2 TONU exception
TONU fee applies unless Customer provides written notice at least 24 hours prior to pickup. This provision constitutes the parties' agreed exception to waiver of accessorial charges in cases where a truck is ordered and not used.

### 6.3 Invoicing of accessorials
All accessorial charges will appear as separate line items on invoices. Documentation supporting accessorial charges will be made available upon reasonable request.

## 7. Late Delivery Remedies and Penalties / Credits

### 7.1 Remedies for late delivery
For late deliveries, the agreed remedy is a flat late delivery fee of $500.00 per late delivery, subject to Carrier defenses for causes outside the Carrier's control.

### 7.2 Applicability
Late delivery fees apply only to actual late deliveries and are not liquidated damages for other forms of loss. Application of the late delivery fee does not preclude Customer from pursuing claims permitted by this agreement for additional direct losses proved to result from Carrier breach.

## 8. Claims, Cargo Liability, and Insurance

### 8.1 Claims
Claims for loss, damage, or shortage must be submitted in writing and supported by reasonable documentation. The parties will follow the claims process set out in incorporated procedures or as otherwise mutually agreed.

### 8.2 Liability cap
Carrier liability for loss of, or damage to, cargo is limited to $50,000.00 per shipment unless a higher declared value and corresponding written agreement are executed prior to carriage.

### 8.3 Insurance
Carrier will maintain insurance customary for the transportation services provided. Upon reasonable request, the Carrier will provide certificates evidencing insurance coverage to the Customer.

## 9. Indemnification

### 9.1 Carrier indemnity
Carrier will defend, indemnify, and hold Customer harmless from third-party claims arising from Carrier's grossly negligent acts or willful misconduct in performing Services under this agreement, subject to the limitations and defenses set forth herein.

### 9.2 Customer indemnity
Customer will defend, indemnify, and hold Carrier harmless from third-party claims arising from Customer's instructions, improper packaging of goods, or Customer's negligence.

## 10. Confidentiality

### 10.1 Confidential information
Each party will keep confidential nonpublic information disclosed by the other party and will not disclose such information except to employees, agents, or contractors who have a need to know and who are bound to confidentiality obligations.

### 10.2 Exceptions
Confidential information does not include information that is or becomes generally known to the public through no fault of the receiving party or which is required to be disclosed by law.

## 11. Termination

### 11.1 Termination for convenience
Either party may terminate this agreement with 60 days' prior written notice to the other party.

### 11.2 Termination for cause
Either party may terminate this agreement for material breach if the breaching party fails to cure the breach in accordance with any cure period agreed in writing or if no cure period is specified, in accordance with applicable law and the remedies provided herein.

### 11.3 Effect of termination
Upon termination, the Carrier will complete any in-transit shipments unless otherwise directed, and the Customer will pay for Services rendered and reasonable wind-down costs as mutually agreed.

## 12. Force Majeure

Neither party will be liable for delays or failures in performance resulting from causes beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, government action, labor disputes, or other events of force majeure. The affected party will notify the other party promptly and use reasonable efforts to resume performance.

## 13. Governing Law and Venue

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois; venue in Cook County, Illinois. The parties agree that the governing law and venue provisions shall govern adjudication of disputes to the extent permitted by law.

## 14. Notices

### 14.1 Methods
All notices required or permitted under this agreement must be in writing and will be deemed given when delivered by hand, sent by nationally recognized courier, or sent by electronic transmission with confirmation.

### 14.2 Addresses
Notices to the Carrier should be sent to the Carrier at its headquarters address listed in Section 1.2. Notices to the Customer should be sent to the address provided on applicable purchase orders or confirmations.

## 15. Miscellaneous

### 15.1 Relationship of the parties
The Carrier and the Customer are independent contractors. Nothing in this agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, employment relationship, or agency beyond the limited agency to arrange for carriage as required by law.

### 15.2 Severability
If any provision of this agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions will continue in full force and effect.

### 15.3 Entire agreement
This agreement, together with incorporated rate sheets, confirmations, and attachments, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes prior agreements relating to the subject matter hereof.
