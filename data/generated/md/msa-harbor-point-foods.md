# Master Service Agreement — Harbor Point Foods, Inc.

## 1. Parties
The Master Service Agreement — Harbor Point Foods, Inc. is between Ridgeline Freight, LLC and Harbor Point Foods, Inc.

### 1.1 Carrier / Provider
Ridgeline Freight, LLC  
1432 Quarrystone Avenue  
Chicago, IL 60632

### 1.2 Customer
Harbor Point Foods, Inc.  
Rockford, IL

## 2. Agreement Date / Term
### 2.1 Effective Date
The effective date of this Master Service Agreement — Harbor Point Foods, Inc. is 04/01/2026.

### 2.2 Term
This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until terminated in accordance with Section 9 below.

## 3. Definitions
Where used in this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:
- "Agreement" means this Master Service Agreement — Harbor Point Foods, Inc. together with all Schedules and Rate Sheets incorporated by reference.
- "Carrier" or "Provider" means Ridgeline Freight, LLC.
- "Customer" means Harbor Point Foods, Inc.
- "Services" means the transportation, handling, pickup, delivery, and related services provided by Carrier under this Agreement for Customer's shipments of packed and frozen groceries and related products.
- "Rate Sheet" means the Carrier's pricing schedule and any written service-specific pricing attached to or incorporated into this Agreement.
- Other capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement shall have the ordinary and customary meanings in the transportation industry.

## 4. Services and Service Levels
### 4.1 Scope of Services
Carrier shall provide transportation and related logistics services as requested by Customer and accepted by Carrier, including pickup, consolidation, carriage, and delivery. Carrier will perform Services in conformance with applicable law and industry standards.

### 4.2 Service Levels
Carrier will use commercially reasonable efforts to meet agreed pickup and delivery windows, maintain temperature control where required, and provide tracking visibility reasonably appropriate to the Services being provided.

### 4.3 Operational Coordination
1. Customer will provide accurate shipment information, labeling, and documentation in a timely manner.
2. Carrier will notify Customer of shipment exceptions and delays as soon as reasonably practicable.
3. Each party will designate operational contacts for day-to-day coordination.

## 5. Rates, Payment Terms and Invoicing
### 5.1 Rates
Services will be billed at the rates set forth in the applicable Rate Sheet, which is incorporated by reference into this Agreement. Rate adjustments shall be effective only if set forth in a written amendment signed by both parties or as provided in the Rate Sheet.

### 5.2 Payment Terms
Payment Terms: Net 30. Customer shall pay undisputed invoices in accordance with Payment Terms: Net 30. All invoices shall be in a form reasonably acceptable to Carrier and shall include sufficient detail to permit Customer to identify the shipment, date, routing, and charges.

### 5.3 Disputes
If Customer disputes any portion of an invoice, Customer shall promptly notify Carrier of the dispute and pay the undisputed portion in accordance with Payment Terms: Net 30. Parties will work in good faith to resolve disputed amounts.

## 6. Accessorial Charges, Exceptions and Credits
### 6.1 Accessorial Charges
Customer shall be responsible for accessorial charges in accordance with the Rate Sheet and Carrier's published practices when applicable, including but not limited to detention, reconsignment, and storage.

### 6.2 Lumper Fees — Exception
Lumper fees charged at actual cost, except for hazmat shipments (third-party lumper charges are Customer responsibility).

### 6.3 Credits
Where Carrier is at fault for a chargeable accessorial event, Carrier will provide reasonable credits or adjustments as appropriate and documented.

## 7. Performance Remedies and Penalties
### 7.1 Remedies
Carrier's primary obligation is to provide timely and careful carriage of shipments. Where Service Levels are not met, the parties agree that remedies will be limited to adjustment or credit of charges for the affected shipment, subject to the limitations set forth in Section 8.

### 7.2 Late Delivery Penalty
The Master Service Agreement — Harbor Point Foods, Inc. imposes a late delivery penalty of 1.5% per week of the affected invoice amount. Any application of this penalty will be limited to the invoice(s) directly affected by the late delivery and shall not create a separate claim for consequential damages.

## 8. Claims, Liability, Insurance and Indemnification
### 8.1 Cargo Liability Cap
Carrier's liability for loss, damage, or shortage to cargo transported under this Agreement shall be limited to $100,000.00 per shipment, unless a higher value is declared, agreed in writing, and additional compensation is paid.

### 8.2 Insurance
Carrier shall maintain liability and cargo insurance customary for the services provided. Upon reasonable request, Carrier will provide certificates of insurance evidencing such coverage.

### 8.3 Claims Procedure
1. All claims for loss or damage to cargo must be submitted in writing with sufficient documentation to support the claim.
2. Carrier will investigate claims promptly and respond as soon as reasonably practicable.
3. Payment or denial of valid claims will be made in accordance with this Agreement and applicable law.

### 8.4 Indemnification
Each party shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the other party from claims arising out of its negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement, subject to the liability limitations and insurance provisions in this Section.

## 9. Termination
### 9.1 Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days prior written notice.

### 9.2 Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breaching party fails to cure the breach within a reasonable period after receipt of written notice specifying the breach.

### 9.3 Effect of Termination
Upon termination, Carrier shall complete any in-transit shipments in a commercially reasonable manner unless otherwise agreed. Customer shall pay Carrier for all Services performed through the effective date of termination.

## 10. Confidentiality
Each party agrees to keep confidential and not disclose to any third party non-public information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or to perform the Services. Confidential information shall be used only for the purposes of performing obligations under this Agreement.

## 11. Force Majeure and Miscellaneous
### 11.1 Force Majeure
Neither party shall be liable for delays or failures in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of nature, labor disputes, government actions, or other similar events.

### 11.2 Compliance with Law
Each party shall comply with applicable laws and regulations governing its performance under this Agreement.

### 11.3 Allocation of Risk
The parties acknowledge the allocation of risk and limits of liability set forth in this Agreement represent a reasonable allocation given the rates and services provided.

### 11.4 Entire Agreement; Amendments
This Agreement, together with any Rate Sheet or Schedules expressly incorporated herein, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter and supersedes all prior communications. No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

## 12. Notices
All notices, requests, consents, claims, demands, waivers and other communications hereunder must be in writing and delivered to the parties at their addresses set forth in Section 1, or to such other address designated by a party in writing. Notices shall be effective upon receipt.

## 13. Governing Law and Venue
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois; venue in Cook County, Illinois.
