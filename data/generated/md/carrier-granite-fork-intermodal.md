# Subcontractor Agreement — Granite Fork Intermodal

## 1. Parties
### 1.1 Identification
This Subcontractor Agreement is made between Ridgeline Freight, LLC and Granite Fork Intermodal. Ridgeline Freight, LLC is identified herein as the "Broker/Shipper" and Granite Fork Intermodal is identified herein as the "Carrier."

### 1.2 Contact Information
Broker/Shipper: Ridgeline Freight, LLC  
Headquarters: 1432 Quarrystone Avenue, Chicago, IL 60632

Carrier: Granite Fork Intermodal  
Carrier principal place of business: Nebraska

## 2. Agreement Date / Term
### 2.1 Effective Date
This agreement is effective as of 03/15/2026.

### 2.2 Term
The agreement continues in effect until terminated in accordance with Section 10 (Term and Termination) below.

## 3. Definitions
For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below unless the context otherwise requires:
- "Agreement" means this Subcontractor Agreement and any appendices or written amendments executed by the parties.
- "Services" means the transportation and related services performed by the Carrier under this Agreement.
- "Cargo" means the goods tendered to Carrier by or on behalf of Broker/Shipper for transportation.
- "Carrier" means Granite Fork Intermodal.
- "Broker/Shipper" means Ridgeline Freight, LLC.
- Other capitalized terms not defined shall be given their plain commercial meaning.

## 4. Scope of Services
### 4.1 Carrier Obligations
Carrier shall provide transportation services for shipments tendered by Broker/Shipper in accordance with the terms of this Agreement using equipment suitable for the load and in compliance with applicable law. Carrier represents that it operates equipment including container chassis and flatbed and will provide equipment that is safe, properly maintained, and appropriate for the load.

### 4.2 Performance Standards
Carrier shall:
1. Accept assignments only if Carrier has the legal authority, capacity, and suitable equipment to perform the Services.
2. Handle Cargo with reasonable care and keep Broker/Shipper informed of delays or incidents materially affecting delivery.
3. Comply with all lawful instructions from Broker/Shipper that are not inconsistent with Carrier's obligations under this Agreement.

## 5. Compliance and Safety
### 5.1 Regulatory Compliance
Carrier warrants that it will comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, rules, and regulations applicable to the transportation of the Cargo, including licensing, permits, and any requirements for the operation of the equipment listed in Section 4.1.

### 5.2 Safety and Screening
Carrier shall maintain reasonable safety programs, driver qualification procedures, and security protocols. Carrier shall not permit drivers or equipment that would reasonably be expected to jeopardize the safe transport of Cargo.

## 6. Fees & Accessorials
### 6.1 Base Freight Charges
Rates for specific shipments will be established by separate rate confirmations, work orders, or shipment tender documents. Those documents, when executed or accepted by Carrier, are incorporated into this Agreement.

### 6.2 Detention and Free Time
Detention at pickup or delivery will be assessed in accordance with the following:
- Detention is charged at $45.00 per hour after 4 hours free time at pickup or delivery.
- Free time at pickup and delivery under this Agreement is 4 hours.

Carrier shall document arrival and release times and provide such documentation upon Broker/Shipper request in support of any detention charges.

### 6.3 Other Accessorials
Any other accessorial charges must be agreed in advance in writing. Carrier must obtain prior written approval from Broker/Shipper for any accessorials that would materially change the cost of a shipment.

## 7. Payment Terms
### 7.1 Invoicing
Carrier shall submit complete invoices with supporting documentation in a timely manner. Invoices must reference Broker/Shipper shipment references and any required proof of delivery.

### 7.2 Payment Timing
Payment for services under the Granite Fork Subcontractor Agreement is due Net 10. Broker/Shipper will pay properly submitted and undisputed invoices in accordance with this term.

### 7.3 Quick-Pay Option
Broker/Shipper may offer a quick-pay option under which Carrier may elect accelerated payment in exchange for a mutually agreed adjustment to the invoice; any such arrangement must be set forth in writing and agreed by the parties.

## 8. Insurance & Liability
### 8.1 Insurance Requirements
Carrier shall, at its expense, maintain and provide certificates of insurance evidencing coverages customary for the services provided, including, without limitation, liability insurance covering the operation of Carrier's vehicles. All insurance policies shall provide that any cancellation or reduction in coverage gives Broker/Shipper reasonable prior written notice when available.

### 8.2 Cargo Liability
Cargo liability will be as follows: Carrier's liability for loss, damage, or shortage to Cargo is limited to $400,000.00 per occurrence, except for hazmat shipments. Carrier may carry higher limits if agreed in writing for particular shipments.

### 8.3 Other Liability
Carrier remains responsible for loss, damage, or delay caused by Carrier's negligence. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to relieve Carrier of its obligations to exercise due care in the handling of Cargo.

## 9. Indemnification and Claims
### 9.1 Indemnity
Carrier shall indemnify, defend, and hold Broker/Shipper harmless from and against claims arising from Carrier's breach of this Agreement, Carrier's negligent or willful acts, or Carrier's failure to comply with applicable laws.

### 9.2 Claims Handling
1. Parties shall notify each other promptly upon learning of any claim involving Cargo loss, damage, personal injury, or third-party property damage arising out of performance of the Services.
2. Carrier shall cooperate in the investigation and defense of claims and shall provide reasonable access to records and personnel.

## 10. Term and Termination
### 10.1 Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience with 45 days' written notice.

### 10.2 Termination for Cause
A party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breaching party fails to cure the breach after receiving reasonable written notice and an opportunity to cure. Termination for cause does not limit any other remedies available at law or in equity.

### 10.3 Effect of Termination
Upon termination, Carrier shall promptly deliver or account for all Cargo in Carrier's possession and submit final invoices for Services performed prior to termination. Broker/Shipper shall pay Carrier for undisputed amounts due for Services performed prior to termination in accordance with the Payment Terms.

## 11. Non-Solicitation and Confidentiality
### 11.1 Non-Solicitation
During the term of this Agreement and for a period of reasonable duration thereafter, the parties shall not directly solicit employees or contractors of the other party for the provision of similar services without the other party's prior written consent.

### 11.2 Confidential Information
Each party shall keep confidential all non-public business information disclosed by the other party in connection with this Agreement and shall use such information solely for performance under this Agreement.

## 12. Force Majeure; Remedies
### 12.1 Force Majeure
Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure to perform hereunder due to causes beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, labor disputes, governmental acts, or other circumstances reasonably beyond the affected party's control; the affected party shall notify the other promptly and use reasonable efforts to mitigate the effect of the event.

### 12.2 Remedies
The rights and remedies available to the parties under this Agreement are cumulative and in addition to any other rights at law or in equity. Monetary damages shall be the primary remedy for loss arising under this Agreement except where injunctive relief is appropriate to prevent irreparable harm.

## 13. General Provisions
### 13.1 Assignment
Carrier shall not assign or subcontract its duties hereunder except to entities authorized in writing by Broker/Shipper. Broker/Shipper may assign this Agreement without Carrier consent to an affiliate or successor in interest.

### 13.2 Relationship of the Parties
Carrier is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent, or partner of Broker/Shipper for any purpose. Carrier shall be solely responsible for payroll taxes, worker classification, and related obligations for its drivers and employees.

### 13.3 Governing Law and Venue
Governing law: Illinois; venue: Cook County, Illinois.

### 13.4 Severability; Waiver; Entire Agreement
If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid, the remainder shall remain in full force. Failure to enforce any provision shall not be construed as a waiver. This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes prior oral or written agreements related to its subject matter.

## 14. Notices and Signatures
### 14.1 Notices
Notices required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, courier, or other commercially reasonable method to the parties at their principal addresses. Notice is effective upon receipt.

### 14.2 Execution
This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Execution by electronic signature, PDF, or signature page transmitted by electronic means will have the same force and effect as an original signature.
