# Carrier Agreement — Ashgrove Haulage LLC

## 1. Parties
This Carrier Agreement is between Ridgeline Freight, LLC and Ashgrove Haulage LLC. Ridgeline Freight, LLC (the "Broker" or "Shipper") is headquartered at 1432 Quarrystone Avenue, Chicago, IL 60632. Ashgrove Haulage LLC (the "Carrier") operates motor carrier services and provides the equipment and personnel necessary to transport freight as contracted under this Agreement.

## 2. Agreement Date / Term
### 2.1 Effective Date
This Agreement is effective as of 02/15/2026 (the "Effective Date").

### 2.2 Term
This Agreement will continue in effect until terminated in accordance with Section 11 (Term and Termination).

## 3. Definitions
For purposes of this Agreement:
1. "Broker" or "Shipper" means Ridgeline Freight, LLC.
2. "Carrier" means Ashgrove Haulage LLC.
3. "Shipment" means a transport of goods requested by the Broker and accepted by the Carrier.
4. "Bill of Lading" means a transport document issued for a Shipment that identifies the goods, parties, and terms of carriage.
5. "Force majeure event" means an event beyond a party's reasonable control that prevents performance.

Defined terms used in this Agreement have the meanings set forth above unless the context requires otherwise.

## 4. Scope of Services
### 4.1 Services Provided
The Carrier shall provide motor carrier transportation services for freight tendered by the Broker in accordance with this Agreement. The Carrier will carry shipments only when rates, lanes, and schedules are mutually agreed in writing or via confirmed electronic tender.

### 4.2 Independent Contractor
The Carrier performs services as an independent contractor and is solely responsible for direction and control of its employees, drivers, and subcontractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, or employment relationship between the parties.

## 5. Carrier Representations & Compliance
The Carrier represents and warrants that it:
1. Is properly authorized to operate as a motor carrier for-hire and will maintain all required operating authority and licenses as applicable.
2. Will comply with all applicable laws, rules, and regulations related to the provision of transportation services and the operation of vehicles.
3. Will ensure drivers are properly licensed, qualified, and fit for duty.
4. Will handle cargo and documentation in accordance with industry standards and good commercial practices.

The Carrier shall ensure that all subcontractors engaged to perform services under this Agreement are bound to comply with the same obligations.

## 6. Equipment and Operations
### 6.1 Equipment
The Carrier represents that it operates the following equipment types: dry van, straight truck. The Carrier shall provide equipment that is safe, well maintained, and suitable for the freight tendered.

### 6.2 Loading and Unloading
Responsibility for loading and unloading will be as agreed on a per-shipment basis. Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the Carrier is responsible for securing freight in-transit once loaded and for following any special handling instructions provided by the Broker.

### 6.3 Dispatch and Communication
Carrier will accept tenders and provide confirmations, estimated times of arrival, and status updates as reasonably requested by Broker. Carrier shall promptly notify Broker of any events that materially affect scheduled pickup or delivery.

## 7. Fees & Accessorials
### 7.1 Detention and Free Time
Free time at pickup and delivery under this Agreement is 3 hours. Detention under the Ashgrove Carrier Agreement is charged at $35.00 per hour after 3 hours free time. Detention charges accrue for delays beyond free time at the pickup or delivery location and are payable in accordance with Section 9 (Payment Terms).

### 7.2 Other Accessorials
Accessorial services (including, without limitation, reconsignment, layovers, special equipment, or additional stops) must be pre-authorized in writing by Broker. Charges for authorized accessorials will be billed in accordance with the accepted rate confirmation for the Shipment.

### 7.3 Billing Documentation
Carrier shall provide itemized invoices that reference the Broker's load confirmation number and include supporting documentation for accessorial charges, detention, and other fees as requested.

## 8. Insurance & Liability
### 8.1 Insurance Requirements
Carrier shall maintain, at its expense, insurance coverage required by law and as reasonably necessary for the services provided. Carrier will provide certificates of insurance upon Broker's request.

### 8.2 Cargo Liability
Carrier's cargo liability is limited to $60,000.00 per occurrence, except for hazmat shipments. For shipments excluded from this limitation by applicable law or agreement (including hazmat shipments), liability will be determined in accordance with applicable law or the written terms specifically agreed for that Shipment.

### 8.3 Claims and Loss
Carrier shall notify Broker promptly of any loss, damage, or shortage claims and shall cooperate in the investigation and resolution of claims. Carrier shall comply with Broker's reasonable requests for documentation and access to evidence relevant to any claim.

## 9. Payment Terms
### 9.1 Standard Terms
Payment for services under the Ashgrove Carrier Agreement is due Net 45. Carrier's invoices must reference the Broker's load confirmation number and be accompanied by proof of delivery and any other required documents. Payments will be made in U.S. dollars by the method agreed between the parties.

### 9.2 Quick-Pay Option
A quick-pay option may be available upon the parties' mutual written agreement that sets forth any adjustments, fees, or other terms associated with accelerated payment. Any quick-pay arrangement must be documented in writing.

### 9.3 Disputes
If Broker disputes any portion of an invoice, Broker will notify Carrier in writing and the parties will work in good faith to resolve the dispute. Payment of undisputed amounts will proceed in accordance with this Agreement.

## 10. Non-Solicitation and Confidentiality
### 10.1 Non-Solicitation
During the term of this Agreement and for a reasonable period thereafter, neither party will directly solicit for hire the other party's drivers, employees, or contractors who were materially involved in performance under this Agreement, without prior written consent.

### 10.2 Confidentiality
Each party will keep confidential information of the other party in strict confidence and will not disclose such information except as necessary to perform under this Agreement or as required by law.

## 11. Term and Termination
### 11.1 Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience with 15 days' written notice.

### 11.2 Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate this Agreement for cause if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within a reasonable period following written notice, or immediately if required by law or where continued performance would create an imminent safety risk.

### 11.3 Effect of Termination
Upon termination, Carrier will promptly deliver outstanding freight to the agreed destination or as instructed by Broker, and Broker will pay Carrier for all authorized services performed and unpaid through the date of termination in accordance with Section 9.

## 12. Indemnification and Risk Allocation
Carrier shall defend, indemnify, and hold Broker harmless from claims arising from Carrier's negligent acts or omissions, or from breaches of Carrier's obligations under this Agreement. Broker shall indemnify Carrier for claims resulting from Broker's negligence or breach. Nothing in this Agreement limits indemnities required by applicable law.

## 13. Force Majeure
Neither party will be liable for delays or failures to perform caused by a force majeure event. The affected party will notify the other party as soon as reasonably practicable and will use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

## 14. General Provisions
1. Governing Law and Venue: Governing law: Illinois; venue: Cook County, Illinois.
2. Assignment: Carrier may not assign this Agreement without Broker's prior written consent, except Carrier may hire subcontractors provided Carrier remains responsible for performance.
3. Severability: If any provision is found invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions will continue in full force and effect.
4. Entire Agreement: This Agreement, together with confirmed load tenders and written rate confirmations, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter and supersedes prior negotiations and writings.

## 15. Notices
All notices required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the parties at their primary addresses or to such other address as a party may designate in writing. Notices are effective upon receipt.

## 16. Claims Handling Procedure
Carrier and Broker agree to the following claims procedure:
1. Carrier will notify Broker of any loss or damage within a reasonable time after discovery.
2. Carrier will provide documentation requested by Broker to support the claim.
3. Broker and Carrier will cooperate in the investigation and settlement of claims.
4. Claims resolution will proceed consistent with applicable law and the limitations of liability set forth in Section 8.2.

## 17. Safety and Operational Audits
Broker may, upon reasonable notice, conduct audits of Carrier's safety compliance and operational records as they relate to performance under this Agreement. Carrier shall provide access to relevant records and information, subject to confidentiality constraints.

## 18. Miscellaneous
1. Amendments to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both parties.
2. No waiver of any breach or right is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.
3. The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.
