# SOP — Inbound Receiving and Dock Scheduling

## 1. Purpose
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) establishes Ridgeline Freight, LLC policies and procedures for inbound receiving and dock scheduling at Ridgeline Freight facilities. The SOP defines hours, appointment requirements, accessorial charges, driver responsibilities, operational steps, exceptions, escalation paths, recordkeeping, and performance measures to ensure safe, efficient, and auditable inbound flow.

## 2. Scope
This SOP applies to all inbound shipments delivered to Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Chicago West and Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Joliet, all carriers contracted to deliver to those locations, and to internal Ridgeline Freight personnel involved in scheduling, receiving, and dock management. It covers routine inbound deliveries, carrier arrival and check-in, unloading, and reporting of accessorial charges.

## 3. Definitions
- Carrier: Any third-party transportation provider delivering freight to Ridgeline Freight facilities. Examples referenced in this SOP include Brightwater Carriers LLC and Ashgrove Haulage LLC.
- Consignee: The Ridgeline Freight business unit or customer receiving inbound goods at a Ridgeline Freight facility.
- Detention: Time a carrier spends waiting beyond allowed free unloading time while on a dock or on property under company control.
- Dock Appointment: A scheduled time slot allocated by Ridgeline Freight Operations for a truck to arrive and be received.
- Lumper: Third-party personnel engaged to perform unloading service on behalf of the carrier or shipper.
- TONU: Truck Ordered Not Used; fee assessed when a dispatched truck is not utilized per Ridgeline Freight scheduling rules.
- Operations: Ridgeline Freight operations staff responsible for dock scheduling and receiving activities.
- Hours-of-Service (HOS): Regulatory limits on driver on-duty and driving time. Drivers and carriers must follow applicable HOS laws.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities
- Operations Manager (Receiving): Authorizes dock appointments, assigns docks, logs arrivals, and documents accessorial charges.
- Dock Supervisors: Direct onsite receiving, confirm cargo condition, supervise lumper activity, and report delays.
- Scheduling Coordinator: Receives appointment requests, confirms appointment windows, and issues appointment confirmations.
- Carrier / Driver: Presents required paperwork, follows arrival procedures, complies with safety and HOS requirements, and advises Operations when thresholds are approached.
- Customer Service: Communicates exceptions and special handling instructions from customers such as Harbor Point Foods, Inc.
- Finance: Reviews accessorial charge records and processes billing.

## 5. Receiving — Hours
Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Chicago West inbound receiving hours are 08:00 to 18:00, excluding federal holidays. Appointments will normally be scheduled within those hours. Exceptions for receiving outside normal hours require prior approval from the Operations Manager and must be documented in the appointment record.

## 6. Receiving — Free Time
- Carriers have 60 minutes of free time for unloading at Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Chicago West. Time on dock is measured from assigned dock arrival until the loaded equipment is released from the dock.
- If operational constraints require queuing measures, Operations will notify carriers and document waiting time in the receiving log.

## 7. Scheduling — Appointment Notice
- Dock appointment requests for Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Joliet must be submitted at least 48 hours before the scheduled inbound delivery.
- Appointment requests should be sent to the Scheduling Coordinator through the designated corporate scheduling channel. Scheduling will confirm appointments in writing. If an appointment cannot be confirmed, alternative arrangements will be proposed.

## 8. Accessorial Charges
Accessorial charges are assessed according to the circumstances described below. All fees are subject to validation and documentation.

### 8.1 Detention
- A detention fee of $75.00 per hour will be charged after the free unloading time at Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Chicago West.
- Detention accrual begins when free unloading time expires and continues until the carrier equipment is released or otherwise authorized by Operations.

### 8.2 Lumper
- Ridgeline Freight assesses a standard third-party lumper fee of $125.00 on inbound shipments, except for Harbor Point Foods, Inc.
- If lumper services are required, the lumper provider will be recorded on the unload ticket and the fee will be applied per the customer or carrier agreement.

### 8.3 TONU
- Ridgeline Freight applies a Truck Ordered Not Used (TONU) fee of $250.00 if a dispatched truck is canceled with less than 2 hours' notice.
- TONU will be charged when an appointment has been confirmed and the carrier fails to use the scheduled slot without the required notice.

## 9. Safety — Driver Requirements
- Drivers must comply with hours-of-service requirements and notify Operations when approaching hours-of-service limits.
- All drivers must follow onsite safety rules, use assigned personal protective equipment when required, and comply with instructions from Dock Supervisors.
- Any safety incident must be reported immediately to the Dock Supervisor and logged in the incident register.

## 10. Procedure — Step-by-Step
These numbered steps govern inbound receiving and dock scheduling from appointment request through completion.

1. Appointment Request and Confirmation
   - Carrier or shipper submits a dock appointment request to the Scheduling Coordinator. For Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Joliet the request must be submitted at least 48 hours before the scheduled inbound delivery.
   - Scheduling confirms or proposes an alternate slot and records the appointment details.

2. Pre-Arrival Communication
   - Operations issues an appointment confirmation with dock assignment and arrival instructions. Confirmed appointments are logged.

3. Arrival and Check-In
   - Upon arrival, the driver reports to the designated check-in point and presents paperwork. The Dock Supervisor records arrival time and dock assignment.
   - For Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Chicago West, free unloading time begins at the recorded arrival-to-dock time and is subject to the 60 minutes of free time for unloading at Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Chicago West.

4. Unloading
   - Dock personnel or an approved lumper performs unloading. If a lumper is used, the standard third-party lumper fee of $125.00 on inbound shipments, except for Harbor Point Foods, Inc., applies and is recorded.

5. Detention and Overages
   - If unloading extends beyond the free unloading time, Operations records detention time and applies the detention fee of $75.00 per hour after the free unloading time at Ridgeline Freight Warehouse - Chicago West.

6. TONU Cases
   - If a dispatched truck is canceled with less than 2 hours' notice, the TONU fee of $250.00 will be assessed according to the confirmed appointment record.

7. Documentation and Billing
   - Dock Supervisors complete unload tickets and record any accessorials. Records are retained in the receiving system for auditing and charging.

8. Post-Unload Release
   - Once equipment is released, Operations records release time and closes the appointment. Any exceptions or disputes are recorded for escalation.

## 11. Exceptions and Escalation
- Exceptions to standard hours, appointment notice, or fees must be approved by the Operations Manager and documented in the appointment record.
- Disputes over accessorial charges are escalated to the Operations Manager and Finance for review. If internal resolution is not reached, escalation proceeds to the Chief Operating Officer.
- For inbound shipments from Harbor Point Foods, Inc., the standard third-party lumper fee of $125.00 on inbound shipments does not apply; this exception will be honored when documented by Customer Service.

## 12. Records and Key Performance Indicators
Ridgeline Freight maintains records of appointment requests, confirmations, arrival and release times, unload tickets, lumper bills, and accessorial invoices. Key performance indicators (KPIs) monitored include on-time arrivals, average dwell time, detention occurrences, and accessorial dispute rate. Records are retained in accordance with corporate retention policy.
